# Where can I find and download completed contracts?

Completed agreements remain in your organisation's **Contracts** area. They do not move to a separate external service after signing.

## Find the agreement

Open **Contracts** and search by the contract title or a party. You can also use the available status and date filters to narrow the list. Open the result and confirm that its status is signed or completed.

If the contract still shows as pending, review its signees and activity history. At least one required recipient may still need to act before the completed file is generated.

## Download the full record

Use the available download action from the completed contract. The PDF includes the agreement and an appended signature certificate containing evidence from the signing flow. Keep the certificate with the agreement rather than separating the pages.

Open the downloaded file to confirm that all contract pages and the certificate are present and readable. Store the file according to your organisation's retention and access process. The contract also stays available in Scriboflow while your organisation retains it.

For full steps, see [Download the completed contract and signature certificate](/help/sending-and-signing/download-the-completed-contract-and-signature-certificate). If a preview or PDF fails, see [The contract preview or PDF is not loading](/help/troubleshooting/contract-preview-or-pdf-is-not-loading).
