# Cancel or reactivate your subscription

Only the organisation owner can cancel or reactivate its subscription.

## Cancel the subscription

1. Open **Settings** and select **Billing**.
2. Select **Manage subscription**.
3. Select **Cancel subscription**.
4. Review the end date and confirm the cancellation.

The subscription remains active until the end of the current paid period. The organisation then becomes read-only. Existing contracts, templates and files remain available, but new creates are blocked.

## Undo a scheduled cancellation

Before the subscription ends, open the Billing page and select **Undo cancellation**. The current subscription then continues beyond the displayed renewal date.

## Reactivate after the subscription ends

To restore full access after the organisation has become read-only, open **Manage subscription**, choose a plan and complete the checkout. The organisation's existing data is preserved.
