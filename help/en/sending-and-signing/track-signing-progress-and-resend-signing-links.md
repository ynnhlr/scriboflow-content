# Track signing progress and resend signing links

The contract status and activity history show where a sent contract is in the signing flow. Check them before following up so you contact the right recipient.

## Review progress

Open the contract from **Contracts** and review its current status, signees and activity. The history records sending and recipient events, while the signee information shows who is still awaiting action.

If signing order is enabled, later signees may be waiting for an earlier person rather than missing an email. Do not resend to someone who is not yet active in the sequence.

## Resend a signing link

When the current recipient needs another email:

1. Open the sent contract.
2. Find the relevant signee and available resend action.
3. Confirm the recipient's email address.
4. Select the action to resend the signing link.
5. Check the activity history for the new event.

Tell the recipient to use the newest Scriboflow email. Never copy one person's secure link to another recipient or publish it in a shared channel.

Automatic reminder timing is configured separately. See [Manage signing deadlines and reminders](/help/creating-and-managing-contracts/manage-signing-deadlines-and-reminders).

## If the link still fails

Confirm that the contract remains active and that the person is the current intended recipient. A link may be invalid, expired or inactive after the contract changes state. Follow [A signing link is invalid, expired or inactive](/help/troubleshooting/signing-link-is-invalid-expired-or-inactive) rather than repeatedly sharing an old link.

When contacting support, include the contract title or ID, recipient email, approximate resend time and visible error—never the active secure link.
