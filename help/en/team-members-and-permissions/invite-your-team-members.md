# Invite your team members

Organisation owners and administrators can invite colleagues to join their Scriboflow organisation.

## Send an invitation

<!-- help-image:image -->
![Settings, organization](../../assets/invite-your-team-members/image-v1.webp)

1. Open **Settings** from your account menu.
2. Select **Organisation**.
3. Go to **Members**.
4. Enter your colleague’s email address.
5. Select **Invite member**.

Scriboflow sends an invitation email and adds it to **Pending invites** until it is accepted.

## Accept an invitation

The recipient opens the link in the invitation email and signs in or creates an account using the invited email address. They then select **Accept invitation**.

After accepting, they appear under **Members** and join the organisation with the Member role.

## Manage pending invitations

Pending invitations show their expiry date. You can select **Resend** to send a new link or **Revoke** to make the invitation link stop working.

If an invitation has expired, invite the colleague again using the same email address.
