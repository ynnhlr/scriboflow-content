# Compare Scriboflow plans

Scriboflow offers Essential, Pro and Enterprise plans. Each plan applies to one organisation.

## Essential

Essential is intended for one user and includes:

- 10 contracts per month or 120 contracts per year
- 5 templates
- 5 saved clauses
- 5 data fields
- 100 MB of Vault storage
- Up to 3 attachments and 25 MB of attachments per contract
- Standard electronic signing with Scriboflow branding

## Pro

Pro is intended for teams and includes:

- Up to 5 users
- 100 contracts per month or 1,200 contracts per year
- 50 templates
- 50 saved clauses
- 50 data fields
- 10 GB of Vault storage
- Up to 10 attachments and 100 MB of attachments per contract
- Company branding, MitID signing and priority support

## Enterprise

Enterprise provides tailored contract, user, storage and attachment limits. It also includes dedicated onboarding and support. Contact Scriboflow to discuss an Enterprise plan.

## Monthly and annual billing

A monthly plan provides a new contract allowance for each monthly billing period. An annual plan provides the full yearly allowance at the start of the annual billing period.

Open **Settings**, select **Billing**, and then select **Manage subscription** to see current prices and choose a plan.
