## 1. Unlimited Personal Liability of Partners

**IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ BEFORE SIGNING.** This Agreement establishes a general partnership (*interessentskab*, "I/S") governed by the Danish Act on Certain Commercial Undertakings (*Erhvervsvirksomhedsloven*). This structure carries the following consequences, which the Partners expressly acknowledge before entering into this Agreement:

**1.1** Unless expressly and validly limited under applicable law (see Section 1.2), **each Partner is personally, jointly and severally, and without limitation liable for all debts and obligations of the Partnership**, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise, regardless of that Partner's ownership percentage, capital contribution, or degree of involvement in management. This liability is **not capped** at the amount of a Partner's contribution and extends to that Partner's personal, non-Partnership assets.

**1.2** If the Partners intend to limit their personal liability, this Agreement is not the appropriate vehicle. The Partners should instead consider structuring the business as a limited partnership (*kommanditselskab*, "K/S"), in which at least one general partner (*komplementar*) retains unlimited liability while one or more limited partners (*kommanditister*) have liability capped at their registered contribution, or as a limited liability company (e.g., an *anpartsselskab* (ApS) or *aktieselskab* (A/S)) under the Danish Companies Act (*Selskabsloven*). By signing this Agreement, each Partner confirms that it has considered these alternative structures and has elected to proceed on an unlimited liability basis.

**1.3** This Section 1 applies notwithstanding, and takes precedence over, any other provision of this Agreement, and operates together with (not instead of) the specific liability provisions in Section 8.2 (internal authority restrictions), Section 12.2 (admission of new Partners), Section 13.3 (withdrawal), Section 14.2 (death or incapacity), and Section 15.3 (dissolution shortfall).

### 8.2 Internal Restrictions Do Not Bind Third Parties

Any restriction on a Partner's authority to bind the Partnership, whether set out in this Agreement or agreed separately among the Partners, is effective only as between the Partners internally. Such a restriction does **not** limit or affect the rights of a third party dealing with the Partnership in good faith, unless the restriction has been (a) registered with the Danish Business Authority (*Erhvervsstyrelsen*) as part of the Partnership's CVR registration under Section 9, or (b) expressly and specifically communicated in writing to that third party before the relevant transaction. A Partner who exceeds an internal restriction remains liable to the other Partners for any resulting loss, without prejudice to the Partnership's and each Partner's continuing liability to the third party under Section 1.

### 8.3 Signing Authority

## 12. Admission of New Partners

### 12.1 Consent

New Partners may only be admitted by unanimous consent. Terms of admission shall be documented in writing.

### 12.2 Liability for Pre-Existing Obligations

An incoming Partner assumes personal, joint and several liability, together with the existing Partners, for all obligations of the Partnership existing at the date of admission, to the same extent as the existing Partners, unless (a) the incoming Partner and all existing Partners agree otherwise in the written admission agreement referred to in Section 12.1, and (b) the relevant creditor has consented in writing to limit or exclude the incoming Partner's liability for the obligation in question. Absent such creditor consent, any internal agreement limiting the incoming Partner's liability is effective only as between the Partners and does not bind the creditor.

### 12.3 Notice to Material Creditors

## 13. Withdrawal

### 13.1 Notice

A Partner may withdraw by giving **[Number] [Time unit]** written notice.

### 13.2 Buyout

The remaining Partners may purchase the withdrawing Partner's interest at fair market value determined by:
- **[Valuation method]**, or
- **[Valuation method]**

### 13.3 Continuing Liability for Pre-Withdrawal Obligations

A withdrawing Partner remains personally, jointly and severally liable, without limitation, for all obligations of the Partnership incurred prior to the effective date of withdrawal, whether or not then due or matured, unless and until the relevant creditor has released the withdrawing Partner in writing. Withdrawal from the Partnership does not, by itself, discharge or reduce the withdrawing Partner's liability to third parties.

### 13.4 Indemnity from Remaining Partners

Without prejudice to Section 13.3, the remaining Partners (and any incoming Partner admitted to replace the withdrawing Partner's interest) shall indemnify the withdrawing Partner against any amount the withdrawing Partner is required to pay to a creditor in respect of a Partnership obligation incurred prior to withdrawal, except to the extent of the withdrawing Partner's own share of that obligation, calculated by reference to the profit and loss sharing ratios in Section 6.1 as at the date the obligation was incurred.

## 14. Death or Incapacity

### 14.1 Buyout

In case of death or permanent incapacity of a Partner:
- the remaining Partners may purchase the affected Partner's interest
- valuation shall follow Section 13.2

### 14.2 Continuing Liability of the Estate

The estate of a deceased Partner, or an incapacitated Partner (acting through their legal representative, as applicable), remains liable for all obligations of the Partnership incurred prior to the date of death or incapacity, on the same basis as set out in Section 13.3, unless released in writing by the relevant creditor.

### 14.3 Indemnity

Section 13.4 applies correspondingly to indemnify the estate or the incapacitated Partner in respect of Partnership obligations incurred prior to the date of death or incapacity.

## 15. Dissolution

### 15.1 Dissolution

The Partnership may be dissolved by unanimous agreement.

### 15.2 Winding Up

Upon dissolution:
- assets shall be liquidated
- liabilities paid
- remaining proceeds distributed according to ownership percentages

### 15.3 Shortfall Liability

If, upon winding up, the Partnership's assets are insufficient to satisfy its liabilities in full, each Partner remains personally, jointly and severally liable, without limitation, to the Partnership's creditors for the shortfall, in accordance with Section 1.

### 15.4 Internal Allocation of Shortfall

As between the Partners, any shortfall paid by a Partner in excess of that Partner's share shall be allocated among, and reimbursed by, the other Partners pro rata to their profit and loss sharing ratios set out in Section 6.1 (as most recently in effect prior to dissolution), regardless of which Partner(s) the creditor(s) initially pursued for payment. A Partner who has paid more than its allocated share of the shortfall has a right of contribution against the other Partners for the excess.
