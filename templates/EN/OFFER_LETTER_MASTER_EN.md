This Offer Letter (the "Offer") is issued on **[Date]**.

## 1. Parties

**Employer**
- Full legal name: **[Company name]**
- Registered address: **[Address]**
- CVR/ID No.: **[CVR]**

**Candidate**
- Full legal name: **[Company name]**
- Address: **[Address]**
- CPR/ID No.: **[CVR]**

The Employer and the Candidate are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 2. Position

The Employer is pleased to offer the Candidate the position of **[Job title]**. The Candidate shall report to **[Manager or department]** and perform duties reasonably associated with the position.

## 3. Start Date

The anticipated start date is **[Date]**, subject to fulfillment of the conditions set out in Section 8.

## 4. Compensation

### 4.1 Salary

The Candidate shall receive a gross salary of **[Number] [Currency]** per month, payable monthly in arrears.

### 4.2 Bonus (If Applicable)

Eligibility for bonus or variable compensation shall be governed by separate written terms and shall not be guaranteed unless expressly stated.

### 4.3 Benefits (If Applicable)

The Candidate shall be entitled to standard company benefits, including:
- Pension: **[Description]**
- Insurance: **[Description]**
- Other benefits: **[Description]**

## 5. Working Hours

The normal working hours are **[Number]** hours per week, subject to operational requirements and applicable law.

## 6. Holiday and Leave

The Candidate shall be entitled to holiday and statutory leave in accordance with applicable legislation.

## 7. Probation Period (If Applicable)

The employment shall be subject to a probation period of **[Number] [Time unit]**, which may not exceed three months. During the probation period, either Party may terminate the employment with 14 days' written notice, unless the formal Employment Agreement sets a different notice within that limit. This reflects the mandatory cap on probation periods and the default notice under section 2c of the Danish Salaried Employees Act (funktionærloven).

## 8. Conditions of Employment

### 8.1 Conditions

This Offer is conditional upon:
- signing of a formal Employment Agreement;
- verification of identity and right to work;
- receipt of satisfactory references (if applicable); and
- any other lawful background checks (if applicable).

If these conditions are not satisfied, the Offer may be withdrawn.

### 8.2 Legal Basis for Background Checks

Verification of references, right-to-work status, and any other background check under Section 8.1 will be carried out on the legal basis of **[Legal basis for background checks]** (state, for example, the Candidate's consent and/or the Employer's legal obligation to verify right to work), in accordance with the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act (databeskyttelsesloven). Information obtained shall be used solely to assess fulfilment of the conditions in this Section 8 and shall not be retained longer than necessary for that purpose.

### 8.3 Timeframe for Confirmation or Withdrawal

The Employer shall confirm in writing whether the conditions in Section 8.1 are satisfied, or withdraw the Offer, within **[Number] [Time unit]** after the later of (i) the anticipated start date in Section 3 and (ii) the Employer's receipt of the last outstanding information needed to assess those conditions.

## 9. Nature of this Offer

### 9.1 Binding Conditional Offer

Upon the Candidate's written acceptance in accordance with Section 13, this Offer becomes a binding conditional employment relationship on the terms set out herein, pending (i) satisfaction of the conditions in Section 8 and (ii) execution of a formal Employment Agreement reflecting these terms in fuller detail. The formal Employment Agreement shall not derogate from the terms of this Offer to the Candidate's detriment.

### 9.2 Withdrawal After Acceptance

If the Employer withdraws this Offer after the Candidate's acceptance for a reason other than the conditions in Section 8 not being satisfied, the Employer shall compensate the Candidate for reasonable, documented reliance losses directly caused by the withdrawal (such as loss of notice pay following resignation from prior employment in reliance on this Offer), up to a maximum of **[Number] [Time unit]** of the salary stated in Section 4.1.

## 10. Written Particulars

Following commencement of employment, the Employer shall issue the Candidate with a written statement of employment particulars, or the formal Employment Agreement satisfying that requirement, in accordance with the Danish Act on Employment Certificates (lov om ansættelsesbeviser), implementing Directive (EU) 2019/1152. The essential terms required under that Act shall be provided no later than 7 calendar days after the start date, and the remaining required particulars no later than 1 month after the start date.

## 11. Confidentiality

The Candidate shall treat this Offer and any related discussions as confidential.

## 12. Governing Law

This Offer shall be governed by the laws of Denmark.

## 13. Acceptance

To accept this Offer, please confirm acceptance in writing no later than **[Date]**. Failure to accept by this date may result in withdrawal of the Offer.
