## 2. Background

### 2.1 Employment Start

The Employee has been employed by the Employer since **[Date]**.

### 2.2 Separation

The Parties have agreed to terminate the employment relationship under the terms set out in this Agreement.

## 3. Termination of Employment

### 3.1 Termination Date

The employment shall terminate on **[Date]** (the "Termination Date").

### 3.2 Garden Leave

The Employee shall remain employed until the Termination Date unless placed on garden leave.

### 3.3 No Further Relationship

After the Termination Date, no further employment relationship shall exist between the Parties.

## 4. Final Compensation

### 4.1 Payments

The Employer shall pay:
- salary up to the Termination Date
- accrued but unused holiday pay in accordance with applicable law
- any outstanding expenses properly documented
- any additional severance compensation agreed in Section 4.2

### 4.2 Severance Payment (If Applicable)

The Employer shall pay the Employee a total severance package of **[Number] [Currency]**, subject to statutory deductions, itemised as follows:
- ordinary severance / termination compensation (fratrædelsesgodtgørelse): **[Number] [Currency]**
- accrued but unused holiday pay under the Danish Holiday Act (Ferieloven): **[Number] [Currency]**
- compensation for any restrictive covenant maintained under Section 9 (non-compete / non-solicitation): **[Number] [Currency]**
- other payments (specify): **[Number] [Currency]**

### 4.3 Itemisation for Tax Purposes

The breakdown set out in Section 4.2 shall be reflected in the Employee's final payslip and settlement statement, so that each category of payment receives the correct income tax withholding, labour-market contribution (AM-bidrag), and holiday-pay treatment applicable under Danish law. Ordinary severance, accrued holiday pay, and compensation for a restrictive covenant are taxed differently and shall not be paid or reported as a single undifferentiated lump sum.

### 4.4 Tax Compliance

All payments shall be made in accordance with applicable tax regulations, consistent with the itemisation required by Section 4.3.

## 5. Legal Advice and Voluntary Agreement

### 5.1 Opportunity for Independent Advice

The Employee confirms that the Employer has afforded the Employee a reasonable period of at least **[Number] [Time unit]** to review the terms of this Agreement and to obtain independent legal advice before signing.

### 5.2 Contribution to Legal Fees

The Employer shall contribute **[Number] [Currency]** (exclusive of VAT) towards the Employee's reasonable and documented costs of obtaining such independent legal advice, payable directly to the Employee's legal adviser upon the Employer's receipt of an invoice.

### 5.3 Voluntary Agreement

The Employee confirms that this Agreement is entered into voluntarily, without duress or undue influence, and following a genuine opportunity to seek the advice described in Section 5.1.

## 6. Release of Claims

### 6.1 Release

In consideration of the payments described in Section 4, the Employee irrevocably releases the Employer from any and all claims existing and known to the Employee as at the Effective Date and arising out of the employment relationship.

### 6.2 Included Claims

This includes, but is not limited to, claims relating to:
- salary or compensation
- bonuses or commissions
- wrongful termination
- discrimination or harassment
- employment-related benefits

### 6.3 Excluded Claims

This release does not apply to, and the Employee does not waive:
- accrued but unpaid holiday pay and holiday allowance under the Danish Holiday Act (Ferieloven), which the Employer shall pay to the Employee or transfer to the relevant holiday fund (Feriekonto) as required by law;
- any right or protection under the Danish Whistleblower Protection Act (Whistleblowerloven), including the right to make protected disclosures and to be free from retaliation for having done so;
- any other claim that cannot be validly waived under mandatory Danish law.

### 6.4 Claims Not Covered

This release is limited to claims existing and known to the Employee as at the Effective Date. It does not cover claims arising after the Effective Date or claims of which the Employee could not reasonably have been aware at the time of signing.

## 7. Confidentiality

### 7.1 Confidential Information

The Employee shall maintain confidentiality regarding:
- the terms of this Agreement
- confidential business information
- trade secrets and proprietary information

### 7.2 Survival

This obligation continues indefinitely.

## 8. Return of Company Property

The Employee confirms that all Company property, including equipment, documents, data, and access credentials, has been returned or shall be returned no later than the Termination Date.

## 9. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation

### 9.1 Continuation of Existing Restrictions

Any existing non-compete or non-solicitation obligations remain in force in accordance with applicable law, including the Danish Act on Employment Clauses (Lov om ansættelsesklausuler), and prior agreements.

### 9.2 Employer Election on Termination

Where the termination of employment is initiated by the Employer for reasons not attributable to the Employee, any pre-existing non-compete or customer non-solicitation clause shall lapse automatically under the Act on Employment Clauses unless the Employer confirms to the Employee in writing, no later than **[Number] [Time unit]** after the date of this Agreement, that it wishes to maintain the restriction. Any such confirmation shall specify which restriction(s) are maintained and shall trigger the Employer's obligation to pay the compensation required by law for the restriction so maintained.

If any restriction is waived or modified by agreement of the Parties, such waiver or modification must be expressly stated here: **[Specify if applicable]**.

## 10. Non-Disparagement (Optional)

The Parties agree not to make false or defamatory statements about each other following termination.

## 11. Admission of Liability

This Agreement does not constitute an admission of liability by either Party.

## 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 13. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the termination of employment. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 14. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.
