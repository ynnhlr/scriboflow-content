# Invite a team member

Organisation owners and administrators can invite colleagues from the organisation settings. An invitation gives the recipient access to the same Scriboflow organisation after they accept it.

## Send the invitation

Open the members area in your organisation settings and choose **Invite member**. Enter your colleague's email address. Invitations add colleagues as members; an organisation owner can change an active member's role afterward. You cannot invite your own email address or someone who is already a member.

## Ask the recipient to accept

Scriboflow sends an invitation to the address you entered. The recipient should open that invitation and continue with the same email address. Until they accept, the invitation remains in the pending invitations list.

## Manage pending invitations

Owners and administrators can resend or revoke a pending invitation. If the recipient cannot find the email, first confirm that the pending invitation uses the correct address before sending it again.
