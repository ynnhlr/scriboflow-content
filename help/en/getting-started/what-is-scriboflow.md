# What is Scriboflow?

Scriboflow is a contract management platform that helps businesses create, sign and manage their contracts in one place.

You can create a contract in three ways:

- Create one from scratch
- Use a ready-made template
- Upload your own contract

Scriboflow also lets you:

- Store and reuse clauses from your clause library
- Add reusable data fields to contracts
- Securely store frequently used documents, annexes and schedules in the Vault
- Add parties, signers and attachments
- Send contracts for review and electronic signature
- Use qualified electronic signatures compliant with eIDAS
- Track contract statuses and expiration dates
- Keep signed contracts organised in a central archive

External parties can review and sign contracts through a secure link without creating a Scriboflow account.

Scriboflow guides you through the complete contract process—from creation to signature and ongoing management.
