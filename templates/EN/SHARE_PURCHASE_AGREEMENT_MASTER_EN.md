## 2. Background

### 2.1 Ownership

The Seller is the legal and beneficial owner of the shares described in Section 3.

### 2.2 Intent

The Buyer wishes to purchase such shares on the terms set out in this Agreement.

## 3. Sale of Shares

### 3.1 Sale

The Seller agrees to sell and transfer to the Buyer **[Number]** shares in **[Company name]**, representing **[Number]%** of the share capital (the "Shares").

### 3.2 Title (Fundamental Warranty)

The Shares are sold free from any lien, encumbrance, or third-party right, except as Disclosed (as defined in Section 7.5) in Schedule 1 (Disclosure Schedule). This warranty is a Fundamental Warranty for the purposes of Section 7.2, is given unconditionally and without qualification by reference to Seller's Knowledge, and is not subject to the De Minimis, the Basket, or the Liability Cap set out in Section 9 (save that it remains subject to the fair disclosure standard in Section 7.5).

## 4. Purchase Price

### 4.1 Price

The total purchase price for the Shares is **[Number] [Currency]** (the "Purchase Price").

### 4.2 Pricing Mechanism

The Parties shall select one of the following pricing mechanisms and complete Schedule 2 accordingly. The provisions of the mechanism selected shall apply to the exclusion of the other.

**(A) Option 1 — Locked Box**

Where the Parties select the Locked Box mechanism:
- the Purchase Price is fixed by reference to the Company's balance sheet as at **[Date]** (the "Locked Box Date") and is not adjusted after Closing, save as set out in this Section 4.2(A);
- the Seller warrants that, during the period from the Locked Box Date to the Closing Date (the "Locked Box Period"), no Leakage has occurred or will occur, other than Permitted Leakage;
- "Leakage" means any dividend or distribution declared, paid, or made by the Company; any payment, asset transfer, assumption or waiver of a liability, or grant of security in favour of the Seller or a person connected with the Seller; any management, monitoring, advisory, or similar fee paid to the Seller or a connected person; any bonus, retention, or transaction payment awarded to the Seller or a connected person; and any cost or expense of the Seller borne by the Company; in each case other than Permitted Leakage;
- "Permitted Leakage" means the payments, if any, listed in Schedule 2, and any Leakage agreed in writing by the Parties after the Locked Box Date;
- if Leakage other than Permitted Leakage occurs during the Locked Box Period, the Seller shall pay the Buyer an amount in **[Currency]** equal to such Leakage on a **[Currency]**-for-**[Currency]** basis (the "Leakage Indemnity"), and the Leakage Indemnity shall not be subject to the De Minimis, the Basket, or the Liability Cap in Section 9.

**(B) Option 2 — Completion Accounts**

Where the Parties select the Completion Accounts mechanism:
- the Buyer shall prepare, or procure the preparation of, draft accounts of the Company as at the Closing Date (the "Completion Accounts") within **[Number] [Time unit]** after the Closing Date, applying the accounting policies and adjustment formula set out in Schedule 2;
- the Seller shall have **[Number] [Time unit]** from receipt of the draft Completion Accounts to review and, where applicable, dispute them by written notice specifying the disputed items and the reasons for the dispute;
- if the Parties do not agree any disputed item within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the Seller's notice of dispute, the matter shall be referred to an independent firm of accountants appointed by agreement of the Parties or, failing agreement, appointed by **[Name]** (the "Independent Expert"), whose determination shall, absent manifest error, be final and binding on the Parties and whose costs shall be shared as set out in Schedule 2;
- the Purchase Price shall be adjusted **[Currency]**-for-**[Currency]** by reference to the difference between the Completion Accounts and the target set out in Schedule 2, and any resulting adjustment shall be paid within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the Completion Accounts becoming final.

**(C) Earn-Out**

Where the Parties agree that part of the Purchase Price is payable as an earn-out pursuant to Schedule 2 (an "Earn-Out"), the Earn-Out shall be calculated and paid as set out in Schedule 2, and Section 10.4 (Earn-Out Protective Covenants) shall apply in addition to, and not in place of, the mechanism selected under Section 4.2(A) or 4.2(B).

### 4.3 Payment

The Purchase Price shall be paid:
- in full on the Closing Date, or
- in accordance with Schedule 2 (Instalments / Escrow / Earn-Out) and Section 4.2

### 4.4 Payment Method

Payment shall be made to the Seller's designated bank account.

## 5. Conditions Precedent to Closing

### 5.1 Conditions

Closing is conditional upon satisfaction, or waiver in accordance with Section 5.4, of the following conditions precedent (the "Conditions"):
- **Merger Control Clearance**: any competent merger control or competition authority with jurisdiction over the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, including, where applicable, the Danish Competition and Consumer Authority (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen) and any other authority listed in Schedule 3, having granted clearance, whether by unconditional approval, approval subject to conditions reasonably acceptable to the Buyer, or expiry of the applicable waiting period without objection, in each case to the extent required by applicable law;
- **Third-Party Consents**: all consents, approvals, and waivers listed in Schedule 3 as required to be obtained prior to Closing, including any change-of-control consents required under material contracts or financing arrangements, having been obtained on terms reasonably satisfactory to the Buyer;
- **No Material Adverse Change**: no event having occurred between the Effective Date and Closing that constitutes a material adverse change in the business, assets, or financial condition of the Company;
- **Bring-Down of Warranties and Disclosure**: the Seller's representations and warranties in Section 7 being true and accurate as at Closing as if repeated on that date, and Schedule 1 (Disclosure Schedule) having been updated and brought down in accordance with Section 7.5(c).

### 5.2 Long-Stop Date

If the Conditions have not been satisfied or waived on or before **[Expiry date]** (the "Long-Stop Date"), either Party may terminate this Agreement in accordance with Section 14.2, provided that the terminating Party is not itself in material breach of its obligations under this Section 5.

### 5.3 Reasonable Efforts

Each Party shall use reasonable efforts, and shall cooperate with the other Party, to satisfy the Conditions as promptly as reasonably practicable, including by making all necessary filings and notifications with, and providing all information reasonably requested by, any competent authority.

### 5.4 Waiver

A Condition set out in Section 5.1, other than Merger Control Clearance, may be waived in whole or in part by written agreement of the Parties or, where a Condition is expressed to be for the sole benefit of one Party, by that Party in writing.

## 6. Closing

### 6.1 Closing Date

Subject to satisfaction, or waiver in accordance with Section 5.4, of the Conditions set out in Section 5, Closing shall take place on **[Date]** (the "Closing Date").

### 6.2 Deliveries

On the Closing Date:
- the Seller shall deliver signed share transfer documents
- the Seller shall deliver the updated Disclosure Schedule in accordance with Section 7.5(c)
- the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price
- the Company shall update its shareholder register

## 7. Representations and Warranties of the Seller

### 7.1 Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, "Seller's Knowledge" means the actual knowledge of **[Name]** and, where applicable, **[Name]**, after each such individual has made reasonable enquiry of the officers and senior employees of the Company who could reasonably be expected to have knowledge of the relevant matter, and excludes any knowledge that would only be imputed or constructive.

### 7.2 Fundamental Warranties (Title and Capacity)

The Seller represents and warrants, on an unqualified basis, without reference to Seller's Knowledge, and subject only to matters Disclosed in accordance with Section 7.5, that (the "Fundamental Warranties"):
- the Seller has full legal capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement and to sell the Shares;
- the Seller is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Shares;
- the Shares are fully paid and free from any lien, encumbrance, or third-party right, except as Disclosed;
- this Agreement constitutes the valid, binding, and enforceable obligations of the Seller.

The Fundamental Warranties are not subject to the De Minimis (Section 9.2), the Basket (Section 9.3), or the Liability Cap (Section 9.5), and survive for the period set out in Section 9.4(a).

### 7.3 Tax Warranties

Subject to matters Disclosed in accordance with Section 7.5, the Seller represents and warrants that (the "Tax Warranties"):
- the Company has duly and timely filed all tax returns required by applicable law and has paid all taxes due;
- to the Seller's Knowledge, the Company is not subject to any ongoing or threatened tax audit, investigation, or dispute;
- to the Seller's Knowledge, the Company has not entered into any tax avoidance arrangement outside the ordinary course of business.

The Tax Warranties survive for the period set out in Section 9.4(b).

### 7.4 General Business Warranties

Subject to matters Disclosed in accordance with Section 7.5, the Seller represents and warrants, to the Seller's Knowledge, that (the "General Business Warranties"):
- the Company is duly incorporated and validly existing under applicable law;
- the Company is not subject to any undisclosed litigation, arbitration, or regulatory proceeding;
- the Company has complied, in all material respects, with applicable laws;
- the Company has disclosed all material liabilities of the Company.

The General Business Warranties survive for the period set out in Section 9.4(c).

### 7.5 Disclosure

- **Fair Disclosure Standard**: "Disclosed" means fairly disclosed in Schedule 1 (Disclosure Schedule) in such manner and with such reasonable detail and specificity as to enable a reasonable buyer, acting diligently, to identify the nature and scope of the matter disclosed and to assess its impact on the Company. A matter is not Disclosed merely because it is referred to in general or ambiguous terms, is capable of being inferred from other Disclosed matters, or could have been discovered through the Buyer's own due diligence, data room review, or public searches, unless it is fairly disclosed as required by this Section 7.5.
- **Effect of Disclosure**: no claim shall lie for breach of a Tax Warranty or a General Business Warranty in respect of any matter fairly Disclosed.
- **Bring-Down**: the Seller shall update and bring down Schedule 1 by written notice to the Buyer promptly upon becoming aware of any matter arising between the Effective Date and Closing that would, if not so notified, cause a Tax Warranty or a General Business Warranty to be inaccurate at Closing. Delivery of an updated Schedule 1 in accordance with this Section 7.5 is a condition to Closing under Section 5.1. Disclosure of a new matter under this Section 7.5 does not limit the Buyer's right to terminate this Agreement under Section 14.2 or to claim damages in respect of a matter that constitutes a material adverse change, but, once accepted by the Buyer in writing or deemed accepted after **[Number] [Time unit]** without objection, it shall qualify the relevant warranty as at Closing.

## 8. Representations and Warranties of the Buyer

The Buyer represents and warrants that:

- the Buyer has sufficient funds to pay the Purchase Price

## 9. Indemnification

### 9.1 Seller Indemnity

The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer for losses arising from breach of the Seller's representations and warranties in Section 7, subject to the De Minimis, the Basket, the survival periods, and the Liability Cap set out in this Section 9.

### 9.2 De Minimis

No claim may be made by the Buyer for breach of a Tax Warranty or a General Business Warranty unless the Buyer's loss in respect of that individual claim (or a series of claims arising from substantially the same facts or circumstances) exceeds **[Number] [Currency]** (the "De Minimis").

### 9.3 Basket

No claim may be made by the Buyer for breach of a Tax Warranty or a General Business Warranty unless and until the aggregate amount of all such claims that individually exceed the De Minimis exceeds **[Number] [Currency]** (the "Basket"), in which case the Buyer may claim the full amount of all such claims and not merely the amount by which they exceed the Basket. The De Minimis and the Basket do not apply to claims for breach of a Fundamental Warranty, to the Leakage Indemnity under Section 4.2(A), or to claims arising from fraud or wilful misconduct.

### 9.4 Survival Periods (Claims Periods)

- **(a) Fundamental Warranties**: claims must be notified within **[Number] [Time unit]** after Closing, or, if longer, within the applicable statutory limitation period under Danish law.
- **(b) Tax Warranties**: claims must be notified within **[Number] [Time unit]** after Closing, reflecting the period during which the Danish tax authorities may reopen or reassess the Company's tax position.
- **(c) General Business Warranties**: claims must be notified within **[Number] [Time unit]** after Closing.
- A claim notified in writing within the applicable period above shall not be extinguished or time-barred solely because legal proceedings have not yet commenced, provided such proceedings are commenced within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the notification.

### 9.5 Liability Cap

Save for the Fundamental Warranties (Section 7.2), the Leakage Indemnity (Section 4.2(A)), and claims arising from fraud or wilful misconduct, none of which is subject to any cap, the Seller's total liability for breach of the Tax Warranties and the General Business Warranties shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

## 10. Covenants

### 10.1 Ordinary Course

From signing until Closing, the Seller shall ensure that the Company conducts business in the ordinary course.

### 10.2 Restrictions

No material changes shall be made without the Buyer's consent.

### 10.3 Leakage Covenant

Where the Locked Box mechanism in Section 4.2(A) applies, the Seller shall procure that, during the Locked Box Period, the Company does not make, agree to make, or permit any Leakage other than Permitted Leakage, and shall notify the Buyer promptly in writing on becoming aware of any actual or potential Leakage.

### 10.4 Earn-Out Protective Covenants

Where an Earn-Out applies pursuant to Section 4.2(C) and Schedule 2, during the earn-out period set out in Schedule 2 (the "Earn-Out Period"), the Buyer shall:
- procure that the Company is operated in the ordinary course of business and in good faith, and shall not take, or cause the Company to take, any action with the intention of reducing or avoiding any Earn-Out payment;
- not, without the Seller's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld), materially change the accounting policies or practices of the Company in a manner that would affect the calculation of the Earn-Out, other than as required by applicable law or generally accepted accounting standards;
- provide the Seller with reasonable access to the financial records and relevant management of the Company for the purpose of verifying the calculation of any Earn-Out payment;
- refer any dispute regarding the calculation of an Earn-Out payment that the Parties are unable to resolve within **[Number] [Time unit]** to an Independent Expert appointed in accordance with Section 4.2(B), whose determination shall, absent manifest error, be final and binding on the Parties.

## 11. Confidentiality

The Parties shall keep confidential all non-public information relating to this transaction.

## 12. Non-Competition (Optional)

The Seller agrees not to compete with the Company for a period of **[Number] [Time unit]** within **[Territory]**, subject to applicable law.

## 13. Taxes and Costs

### 13.1 Costs

Each Party shall bear its own costs related to this transaction.

### 13.2 Transfer Taxes

Any transfer taxes shall be borne by **[Party reference]**, unless otherwise required by law.

## 14. Termination

### 14.1 Pre-Closing Termination

This Agreement may be terminated prior to Closing by mutual written consent.

### 14.2 Termination for Failure of Conditions Precedent

If any Condition set out in Section 5.1 has not been satisfied or waived on or before the Long-Stop Date, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party, provided that the terminating Party has complied with its obligations under Section 5.3 and is not itself in material breach of this Agreement.

### 14.3 Effects

If terminated before Closing, neither Party shall have further obligations except confidentiality, and, for the avoidance of doubt, no liability shall arise from a termination properly effected under Section 14.1 or 14.2, save in respect of any prior breach of this Agreement.

## 15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 16. Severability

If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

## 17. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the sale of the Shares. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 18. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
