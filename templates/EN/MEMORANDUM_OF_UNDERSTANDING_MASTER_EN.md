This shall not limit the non-breaching Party's right to claim additional damages to the extent its actual, documented loss exceeds the liquidated sum, where permitted by applicable law.

## 10. Non-Binding Nature

### 10.1 Binding Sections

Except for Section 7 (Confidentiality), Section 8 (Exclusivity), Section 9 (Remedies for Breach of Confidentiality and Exclusivity), Section 12 (Governing Law and Jurisdiction), and Section 13 (Signatory Authority), this MoU is not legally binding and does not create any obligation on either Party to proceed with the contemplated collaboration.

### 10.2 No Obligation

No Party shall be obligated to proceed with the contemplated collaboration unless a definitive agreement is executed.

### 10.3 Withdrawal from Negotiations; Pre-Contractual Liability

Subject always to the binding sections identified in Section 10.1, either Party may withdraw from the negotiations and discontinue discussions regarding the contemplated collaboration at any time and for any reason, without liability to the other Party. This Section 10.3 is intended to reduce, to the extent permitted by applicable law, the Parties' exposure to liability for breaking off negotiations in bad faith (culpa in contrahendo) under Danish law, although the Parties acknowledge that such liability cannot be excluded entirely. Nothing in this Section 10.3 limits or excludes liability for: (a) breach of the binding sections referred to in Section 10.1; (b) fraud or wilful misconduct; or (c) conduct in the negotiations that constitutes bad faith to an extent that cannot be excluded under mandatory Danish law.

## 11. Term and Termination

This MoU shall remain in effect until the earliest of:
- execution of a definitive agreement;
- written termination by either Party pursuant to Section 10.3; or
- the Long-Stop Date set out in Section 8.2, unless extended in writing by the Parties.

Termination or expiry of this MoU shall not affect the Parties' rights and obligations under Section 7 (Confidentiality), Section 9 (Remedies for Breach of Confidentiality and Exclusivity), and Section 12 (Governing Law and Jurisdiction), all of which shall survive termination or expiry.
