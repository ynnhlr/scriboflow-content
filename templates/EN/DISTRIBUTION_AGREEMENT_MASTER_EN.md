The Supplier and the Distributor are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 2. Appointment

### 2.1 Appointment

The Supplier appoints the Distributor to market and resell the products described in Schedule 1 (the "Products") within the Territory defined below.

### 2.2 Acceptance

The Distributor accepts the appointment and agrees to actively promote and sell the Products.

### 2.3 Exclusivity

This Agreement is **[Exclusivity]** within the Territory.

### 2.4 Independent Reseller Status

The Distributor purchases the Products from the Supplier and resells them in its own name and for its own account. The Distributor bears the full commercial risk of resale, including price risk and credit risk toward its customers, and has no authority to negotiate, conclude, or otherwise bind the Supplier to any contract with a third party. The Parties intend the relationship created by this Agreement to be that of an independent buyer and reseller, and not that of a commercial agent within the meaning of the Danish Commercial Agents Act (Handelsagentloven). Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to entitle the Distributor to any goodwill compensation or indemnity payable to a commercial agent under that Act.

## 3. Territory

### 3.1 Territory

The Distributor is authorised to distribute the Products within **[Territory]** (the "Territory").

### 3.2 Active Sales Outside the Territory

The Distributor shall not actively sell the Products outside the Territory without the Supplier's prior written consent. "Active sales" means actively approaching individual customers by, among other means, direct mail, visits, or advertising and promotion specifically targeted at customers located outside the Territory.

### 3.3 Passive Sales

Passive sales — that is, responding to unsolicited orders from individual customers located outside the Territory, including sales resulting from the Distributor's general advertising or website that is not specifically targeted at customers outside the Territory — may not be restricted, prevented, or penalised under this Agreement. Restricting passive sales is a hardcore restriction under applicable Danish and EU competition law and is not permitted.

### 3.4 Resale Prices

Any price list, recommended price, or suggested resale price communicated by the Supplier is non-binding on the Distributor. The Supplier shall not fix, require, or otherwise seek to impose minimum or fixed resale prices on the Distributor. Such conduct constitutes a hardcore restriction capable of voiding any applicable competition-law exemption for the whole Agreement.

## 4. Orders and Acceptance

### 4.1 Orders

All orders submitted by the Distributor are subject to written acceptance by the Supplier.

### 4.2 Rejection

The Supplier may reject orders at its discretion.

### 4.3 Governing Terms

Each accepted order constitutes a binding sales contract governed by this Agreement and the Supplier's General Terms and Conditions of Sale.

## 5. Prices and Payment

### 5.1 Prices

The Distributor shall purchase Products at the wholesale prices listed in Schedule 2.

### 5.2 Price Changes

The Supplier may adjust prices upon **[Number] [Time unit]** written notice.

### 5.3 Payment Terms

Payment terms are **[Number] [Time unit]** from invoice date unless otherwise agreed.

### 5.4 Late Payment Interest

If the Distributor fails to pay any undisputed amount by its due date, interest accrues on the overdue amount from the due date until payment in full at a rate of **[Interest rate]** per annum.

### 5.5 Recovery Costs

In addition to interest under Section 5.4, the Supplier is entitled to the fixed compensation for debt recovery costs prescribed by the Danish Interest Act (Renteloven), implementing the EU Late Payment Directive, for each overdue invoice. This entitlement cannot be waived by the Distributor in advance.

### 5.6 Suspension of Deliveries

Without prejudice to Sections 5.4 and 5.5, late payment entitles the Supplier to suspend further deliveries until all overdue amounts, including accrued interest and recovery costs, have been paid in full.

## 6. Marketing and Promotion

### 6.1 Distributor Obligations

The Distributor shall:
- actively market the Products in the Territory
- maintain adequate sales resources
- comply with brand guidelines provided by the Supplier

### 6.2 Representations

The Distributor shall not make representations about the Products beyond those approved by the Supplier.

## 7. Minimum Performance (Optional)

### 7.1 Targets

The Distributor shall achieve minimum annual purchase volumes as defined in Schedule 3.

### 7.2 Consequences

Failure to meet minimum targets may result in:
- termination of exclusivity, or
- termination of this Agreement

## 8. Intellectual Property

### 8.1 Ownership

All trademarks, trade names, and branding remain the property of the Supplier.

### 8.2 License

The Distributor is granted a limited, non-transferable right to use such intellectual property solely for marketing the Products during the term of this Agreement.

### 8.3 Post-Termination

Upon termination, all use of intellectual property must cease immediately.

## 9. Warranties and Liability

### 9.1 Supplier Warranty

The Supplier warrants that the Products comply with applicable laws at the time of delivery.

### 9.2 Limitation

The Supplier's liability shall be limited in accordance with its General Terms and Conditions of Sale.

### 9.3 Local Compliance

The Distributor is responsible for compliance with local marketing and resale regulations within the Territory.

## 10. Product Liability

### 10.1 Strict Liability Under the Product Liability Act

Liability toward an injured third party for personal injury or damage to consumer property caused by a defective Product is governed by the Danish Product Liability Act (Produktansvarsloven), under which liability is strict and cannot be excluded or limited by agreement. Depending on the circumstances, the Distributor may itself be treated as a liable producer toward the injured party, for example where the Distributor is the importer of the Products into the EU/EEA.

### 10.2 Allocation Between the Parties

As between the Supplier and the Distributor, and subject to Section 10.3, each Party is liable for damage caused by its own fault.

### 10.3 Supplier Indemnity

The Supplier shall indemnify and hold the Distributor harmless against product-liability claims brought by a third party to the extent the claim results from a defect in the Products existing at the time of delivery, and not caused or contributed to by the Distributor's own act, omission, storage, handling, or modification of the Products.

### 10.4 Insurance

Each Party shall maintain product-liability insurance with a reputable insurer in an amount appropriate to its exposure under this Agreement and shall provide evidence of such cover upon reasonable request.

### 10.5 Notice

The Distributor shall promptly notify the Supplier of any product claims.

### 10.6 Admissions

The Distributor shall not admit liability on behalf of the Supplier without prior written consent.

## 11. Term and Termination

### 11.1 Initial Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of **[Number] [Time unit]**.

### 11.2 Renewal

Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of the same duration unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **[Number] [Time unit]** before the end of the then-current term. This notice period is a minimum floor and shall be applied having regard to the duration of the relationship and the volume of trade built up by the Distributor, so that the Distributor is afforded a reasonable opportunity to wind down or reorganise its business before the Agreement ends.

### 11.3 Immediate Termination

Either Party may terminate immediately in case of:
- material breach not remedied within **[Number] [Time unit]**
- insolvency
- serious violation of brand or competition obligations

## 12. Consequences of Termination

### 12.1 Effects

Upon termination:
- all outstanding payments become due
- the Distributor shall cease representing itself as authorised distributor
- remaining stock handling shall be agreed in writing

### 12.2 Compensation

No goodwill or compensation shall be payable unless required by mandatory law.

## 13. Confidentiality

Each Party shall treat commercial and technical information received from the other Party as confidential.

## 14. Competition Law Compliance

### 14.1 Compliance

The Parties shall comply with applicable competition and antitrust laws, including the rules on vertical agreements under Danish and EU competition law.

### 14.2 Hardcore Restrictions

Without limiting Section 14.1, the Parties confirm that: only active sales by the Distributor outside the Territory may be restricted, as set out in Section 3.2; passive sales may never be restricted, as set out in Section 3.3; and any resale prices communicated by the Supplier are non-binding maximum or recommended prices, as set out in Section 3.4. The Parties shall not implement this Agreement in a manner that constitutes a hardcore restriction capable of voiding any applicable competition-law exemption.

### 14.3 Interpretation

Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted as restricting lawful competition beyond what is permitted under applicable law.

## 15. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform due to events beyond reasonable control.

## 16. Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent, except in connection with a merger or business transfer.

## 17. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

### 17.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules.

### 17.2 Exclusion of the CISG

The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) shall not apply to this Agreement or to any sales contract concluded under it. The Danish Sale of Goods Act (Købeloven) applies as background law between the Parties, to the extent not otherwise provided in this Agreement, and the Parties acknowledge that, as merchants, most of its provisions may be and are hereby varied by this Agreement.

### 17.3 Jurisdiction

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 18. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning distribution of the Products. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 19. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
