## 2. Purpose

### 2.1 Relationship

This Agreement governs the relationship between the Shareholders and the Company.

### 2.2 Articles Alignment

In case of conflict between this Agreement and the Company’s Articles of Association, the Parties shall take necessary steps to align the Articles with this Agreement.

### 2.3 Status of this Agreement vis-à-vis the Company and Third Parties

This Agreement is a contractual arrangement between the Parties. It does not amend, supersede, or override the Company's Articles of Association, and it does not, in itself, bind the Company's corporate organs (including the general meeting and the board of directors) to act, or refrain from acting, in any particular way. As between the Company and third parties, including for the purposes of determining the validity of resolutions passed at a general meeting or by the board of directors and the registration of Shareholders in the Company's register of shareholders, the Articles of Association and applicable law shall prevail. A resolution passed, or action taken, by the general meeting or the board of directors in breach of this Agreement but in accordance with the Articles of Association and applicable mandatory law remains valid and binding on the Company and third parties, and shall not be capable of being set aside solely by reference to this Agreement. Any remedy for such a breach shall be available solely as between the Shareholders on a contractual basis, including a claim for damages and, to the extent available under applicable law, specific performance or injunctive relief, and shall not affect the validity of the corporate action vis-à-vis the Company or third parties. The Parties shall, to the extent permitted by law, promptly propose, table, and vote in favor of any amendment to the Articles of Association that is reasonably necessary to give effect to any provision of this Agreement that is intended to bind the Company or its corporate organs.

Where the Selskabsloven or other mandatory law requires a higher majority than the threshold agreed in this Agreement for a given matter, the statutory majority shall apply, and compliance with the threshold in Section 4.2 shall not, by itself, satisfy or substitute for that statutory requirement.

## 6. Transfer of Shares

### 6.1 Approval

Shares may not be transferred without the prior written approval of Shareholders holding at least **[Number]%** of the votes attaching to the shares, except for a Permitted Transfer under Section 6.3.

### 6.2 Right of First Refusal

Any Shareholder wishing to transfer shares, other than pursuant to a Permitted Transfer under Section 6.3, shall first give written notice to the other Shareholders and the Company (the "Transfer Notice"), stating the number of shares to be transferred, the identity of the proposed transferee, and the proposed price and other material terms. The other Shareholders shall have **[Number] [Time unit]** from receipt of the Transfer Notice to elect, by written notice to the transferring Shareholder and the Company, to purchase the offered shares pro rata to their existing shareholdings, on the same price and terms as set out in the Transfer Notice (the "Right of First Refusal"). If the other Shareholders do not elect to purchase all of the offered shares within such period, the transferring Shareholder may, subject to Sections 6.1 and 6.4, transfer the unpurchased shares to the proposed transferee within **[Number] [Time unit]** thereafter, on terms no more favorable to the transferee than those set out in the Transfer Notice.

### 6.3 Permitted Transfers

Notwithstanding Sections 6.1 and 6.2, the following transfers ("Permitted Transfers") shall not be subject to the approval requirement in Section 6.1 or the Right of First Refusal in Section 6.2, provided that the transferee first executes a deed of accession in accordance with Section 6.4:
- a transfer to an Affiliate of the transferring Shareholder;
- a transfer to a spouse, civil partner, or lineal descendant of the transferring Shareholder, or to a trust, foundation, or holding company established for bona fide estate or succession planning purposes for the benefit of the transferring Shareholder or such persons;
- a transfer occurring by reason of the death of a Shareholder to that Shareholder's estate or heirs, subject to Section 12; and
- any other transfer approved in writing by Shareholders holding at least **[Number]%** of the votes attaching to the shares.

For purposes of this Agreement, "Affiliate" means, in relation to a Shareholder, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Shareholder.

### 6.4 Accession of New Shareholders

No transfer of shares (whether a Permitted Transfer under Section 6.3, a transfer under Sections 6.1-6.2, or a transfer pursuant to Section 7 (Drag-Along) or Section 8 (Tag-Along)), and no issuance of new shares under Section 3.2, shall be valid, registered, or effective vis-à-vis the Company and the other Shareholders unless the transferee or subscriber has, prior to or concurrently with completion of the transfer or issuance, executed and delivered to the Company and the other Parties a deed of accession substantially in the form set out in Schedule 3 (the "Deed of Accession"), pursuant to which the transferee or subscriber agrees to be bound by, and to assume the rights and obligations of a Shareholder under, this Agreement. The board of directors shall refuse to register any transfer or issuance in the Company's register of shareholders where this condition has not been satisfied.

### 6.5 Consequences of Unauthorized Transfers

A transfer of shares in breach of this Agreement (including breach of Section 6.1, 6.2, 6.3, or 6.4, or of Section 7 or 8) shall not, by itself, render the transfer void as a matter of company law. Instead:
- the Company shall refuse to register the purported transferee as a shareholder in the Company's register of shareholders, and the purported transferee shall not be recognized as a Shareholder, or be entitled to exercise any shareholder rights (including voting or dividend rights), vis-à-vis the Company until the requirements of this Agreement have been satisfied;
- the transferring Shareholder shall be liable to the other Parties for any loss caused by the breach; and
- the non-breaching Parties shall be entitled to seek specific performance, injunctive relief, or other equitable remedies available under applicable law to prevent or unwind the transfer, in addition to damages.

## 7. Drag-Along Rights

### 7.1 Trigger

If one or more Shareholders holding in aggregate at least **[Number]%** of the share capital and votes of the Company (the "Dragging Shareholders") agree to sell all, but not less than all, of their shares to a bona fide third-party purchaser that is not an Affiliate of the Dragging Shareholders, the Dragging Shareholders may require the remaining Shareholders (the "Dragged Shareholders") to sell all of their shares to the same purchaser on the same terms and conditions (the "Drag-Along Right").

### 7.2 Price Floor

The consideration payable to each Dragged Shareholder per share shall be no less than the highest price per share, calculated on an as-converted, pro-rata basis, paid or payable to any Dragging Shareholder for shares sold in the same transaction, and shall otherwise be on economic terms no less favorable, pro rata, than those offered to the Dragging Shareholders.

### 7.3 Notice and Process

The Dragging Shareholders shall give written notice (the "Drag-Along Notice") to the Dragged Shareholders no later than **[Number] [Time unit]** prior to the anticipated completion date, setting out the identity of the purchaser, the proposed price and terms, and the anticipated completion date. Each Dragged Shareholder shall execute all documents and take all actions reasonably necessary to give effect to the sale, including entering into a Deed of Accession or transfer documentation as applicable, provided that no Dragged Shareholder shall be required to give representations, warranties, or indemnities beyond those relating to its own title to, and ownership of, its shares and its capacity to enter into the transaction.

### 7.4 Carve-out

The Drag-Along Right shall not apply to, and shall not be triggered by, a Permitted Transfer under Section 6.3.

## 8. Tag-Along Rights

### 8.1 Trigger

If one or more Shareholders (the "Selling Shareholders") propose to sell shares representing more than **[Number]%** of the share capital of the Company in a single transaction or a series of related transactions, other than pursuant to a Permitted Transfer under Section 6.3 or the exercise of a Drag-Along Right under Section 7, each remaining Shareholder (a "Tagging Shareholder") shall have the right to require the Selling Shareholders to procure that the purchaser also purchases, on the same price and terms, that proportion of the Tagging Shareholder's shares as corresponds to the proportion of the Selling Shareholders' shares being sold (the "Tag-Along Right").

### 8.2 Notice and Process

The Selling Shareholders shall give written notice (the "Sale Notice") to the other Shareholders no later than **[Number] [Time unit]** prior to the anticipated completion date, setting out the identity of the purchaser, the number of shares proposed to be sold, and the proposed price and terms. Each Tagging Shareholder shall notify the Selling Shareholders within **[Number] [Time unit]** of receipt of the Sale Notice if it wishes to exercise its Tag-Along Right. If the purchaser is unwilling to purchase all shares tendered by the Tagging Shareholders together with the Selling Shareholders' shares, the Selling Shareholders shall reduce the number of shares they sell so as to permit the Tagging Shareholders to sell their full pro rata entitlement, unless the Tagging Shareholders agree otherwise.

### 8.3 Price Floor

The consideration payable to a Tagging Shareholder per share shall be no less than the price per share paid to the Selling Shareholders in the same transaction.

## 9. Deadlock

### 9.1 Negotiation

In the event of a deadlock on reserved matters, the Parties shall attempt resolution through negotiation.

### 9.2 Mediation

If unresolved within **[Number] [Time unit]**, the matter shall be escalated to mediation.

### 9.3 Binding Shotgun Mechanism

If the deadlock is not resolved within **[Number] [Time unit]** after referral to mediation under Section 9.2, any Shareholder involved in the deadlock (the "Offeror") may deliver a written notice to the other Shareholder(s) involved in the deadlock (the "Offeree(s)") stating a price per share (the "Offer Price") at which the Offeror offers, at the Offeror's election, either (a) to sell all, but not less than all, of the Offeror's shares to the Offeree(s), or (b) to purchase all, but not less than all, of the Offeree(s)' shares (a "Shotgun Notice").

Within **[Number] [Time unit]** of receipt of a Shotgun Notice, the Offeree(s) shall elect, by written notice to the Offeror, either to: (i) sell all of their shares to the Offeror at the Offer Price, or (ii) purchase all of the Offeror's shares at the Offer Price. If the Offeree(s) fail to make an election within such period, the Offeree(s) shall be deemed to have elected to sell their shares to the Offeror at the Offer Price.

Completion of the sale and purchase elected, or deemed elected, under this Section 9.3 shall take place within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the election (or deemed election), against payment of the Offer Price in cleared funds, and the selling Shareholder(s) shall execute all documents reasonably required to give effect to the transfer, including a Deed of Accession where the acquiring party is not already a Shareholder. A transfer completed under this Section 9.3 shall not require compliance with Sections 6.1 or 6.2, but shall remain subject to Section 6.4 (Accession).

### 9.4 Dissolution

If the deadlock relates to a matter for which the mechanism in Section 9.3 is not available, is not exercised by any Shareholder, or does not resolve the deadlock, the Parties may dissolve the Company, subject to applicable law.
