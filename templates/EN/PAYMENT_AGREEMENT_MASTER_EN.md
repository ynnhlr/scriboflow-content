### 6.1 Default Trigger

### 6.2 Prior Notice and Cure Period for a Consumer Debtor

If the Debtor is a Consumer Debtor, the Creditor shall, before accelerating the Outstanding Amount or exercising any other default remedy under Section 6.3, give the Debtor written notice of the missed payment and a cure period of at least **[Number] [Time unit]** (which shall not be shorter than any minimum period required by mandatory Danish consumer-protection law) within which the Debtor may remedy the default. ### 6.3 Remedies

Subject to Section 6.2 where the Debtor is a Consumer Debtor, in case of default:
- the entire remaining Outstanding Amount shall become immediately due and payable
- interest shall continue to accrue on overdue amounts in accordance with Section 4
- the Creditor may charge the mandatory fixed compensation for recovery costs available under Danish law, in addition to interest
- the Creditor may initiate legal recovery proceedings

### 6.4 Cumulative Rights

The Creditor's rights under this Agreement are cumulative and do not waive other remedies available under law.

### 8.2 Safeguards for an Individual Guarantor

Where the security in Section 8.1 includes a personal guarantee (kaution) given by an individual acting outside their trade, business, or profession:
- the guarantee shall state a maximum capped amount, which shall not exceed the Outstanding Amount together with accrued interest and recovery costs permitted under this Agreement;
- before the guarantee is given, the Creditor shall provide the guarantor with clear written disclosure of the nature and extent of the guarantor's potential liability, the amount guaranteed, and the risk that the guarantor may be required to pay if the Debtor defaults;
- the guarantor shall countersign this Agreement or a separate guarantee instrument confirming receipt of that disclosure;
- the guarantor's liability is limited to the capped amount stated in the guarantee and does not automatically extend to any subsequent increase in the Outstanding Amount without the guarantor's express written consent.
