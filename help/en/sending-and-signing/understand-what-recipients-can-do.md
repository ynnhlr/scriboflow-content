# Understand what recipients can do

External recipients open the contract through a secure Scriboflow link. They do not need a Scriboflow account to review and act on the contract sent to them.

## Review and sign

The recipient can read the contract and download the document where that action is available. A signee follows the selected signature method to confirm their signature. Scriboflow records the action in the contract history.

If signing order is enabled, only the current signee can complete the next step. Other recipients receive access when their place in the sequence is reached.

## Request changes

A recipient who cannot accept the current terms can request changes and provide a note. The contract returns to the organisation with a changes-requested status, allowing your team to review the feedback.

Requesting changes is not a signature. Open the request, decide what should change and follow [Respond to requested changes](/help/creating-and-managing-contracts/respond-to-requested-changes) before sending an updated version.

## Reject the contract

A recipient can reject instead of signing. Rejection records that outcome and stops the current signing flow. Review the recipient's information and the contract activity before deciding whether a separate contract should be prepared.

Do not ask recipients to share their signing link with someone else. If a different person should act, handle the participant change from the contract owner side using the actions available for its status.

For link protection, see [Understand secure signing links](/help/security-privacy-and-data/understand-secure-signing-links). If every required signee completes the contract, continue with [Download the completed contract and signature certificate](/help/sending-and-signing/download-the-completed-contract-and-signature-certificate).
