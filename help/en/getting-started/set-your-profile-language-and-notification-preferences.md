# Set your profile, language and notification preferences

Your account settings control your personal Scriboflow experience. Organisation settings control shared behaviour for the workspace you currently have selected.

## Review your profile

Open your account menu and go to your profile or settings. Keep your name and other available account details accurate so colleagues can recognise your activity. Your sign-in email identifies your account, so take care when following links or invitations sent to a different address.

If your account has access to several organisations, confirm the selected organisation before changing shared settings. A profile change applies to you, while an organisation change may affect other members.

## Choose your language

Use the language option in Scriboflow to switch the interface between the available languages. The setting changes navigation and interface text; it does not translate contract terms, templates or content written by your organisation.

Contract and email content should therefore be written in the language your recipients expect. Review the preview before sending instead of relying on the interface language alone.

## Manage notifications

Open the relevant notification settings and review the available email choices for your selected organisation. These can include updates about signing, requested changes, rejection and completion. Enable the messages that help your team follow contracts without creating unnecessary inbox noise.

Notification settings do not replace the contract's activity history. Open the contract when you need a complete record of actions. For account protection, also review [Protect your Scriboflow account](/help/security-privacy-and-data/protect-your-scriboflow-account) and enable multi-factor authentication where available.
