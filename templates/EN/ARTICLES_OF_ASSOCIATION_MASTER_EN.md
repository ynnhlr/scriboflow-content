### 5.2 Rights

- voting rights

### 5.3 Variations

Any variation, restriction, or abrogation of the rights attached to a share class requires a resolution passed with the majority set out in Section 14.1 (Amendments). Where a variation is adverse to the holders of a particular share class, such variation additionally requires the unanimous approval of the shareholders of the affected class, in each case in accordance with the Danish Companies Act (Selskabsloven). Where a resolution under this Section 5.3 increases the obligations of any shareholder, Section 14.2 (Increased Obligations) applies.

## 6. Capital Increases and Decreases

### 6.1 Increase in Share Capital

The share capital may be increased by resolution of the general meeting, adopted with the majority set out in Section 14.1, by way of (i) cash contribution, (ii) contribution in kind, (iii) conversion of debt into share capital, or (iv) issuance of bonus shares, in each case in accordance with the Danish Companies Act. The resolution shall state the terms of the increase, including the number of shares, subscription price, and share class, if any.

### 6.2 Pre-Emption Rights

Upon any increase of the share capital by cash contribution, existing shareholders have a pre-emptive right to subscribe for new shares in proportion to their existing shareholding, unless such right is disapplied or limited by a resolution of the general meeting passed with the majority set out in Section 14.1 and in accordance with the requirements of the Danish Companies Act. Any resolution disapplying or limiting the pre-emptive right shall state the reasons therefor.

### 6.3 Decrease in Share Capital

The share capital may be reduced by resolution of the general meeting, adopted with the majority set out in Section 14.1, for the purpose of (i) covering losses not covered by distributable reserves, (ii) repayment to shareholders, or (iii) allocation to a reserve for future capital increase or distribution, subject to the notice-to-creditors procedure and any other requirements of the Danish Companies Act. The share capital may not be reduced below the statutory minimum referred to in Section 4.4 unless the reduction is carried out simultaneously with an increase that brings the share capital back to at least such minimum.

## 7. Transfer of Shares

### 7.1 Approval

Shares may not be transferred without prior approval of the general meeting unless otherwise provided by law.

### 7.2 Right of First Refusal

Existing shareholders shall have a right of first refusal on any proposed transfer.

### 7.3 Refusal of Transfer — Mandatory Buy-Out

If approval under Section 7.1 is withheld, or if the right of first refusal under Section 7.2 is exercised, the Company shall, within **[Number] [Time unit]** from the date of the refusal or exercise, procure that the shares proposed to be transferred are purchased at fair market value, either by the Company (to the extent permitted by the Danish Companies Act) or by one or more of the remaining shareholders pro rata to their existing shareholdings. Fair market value shall be agreed between the transferring shareholder and the purchaser(s), failing which it shall be determined by an independent expert appointed by the Company's auditor within **[Number] [Time unit]** of a request by either party. If the purchase is not completed within **[Number] [Time unit]** from the date of the refusal or exercise, the transferring shareholder shall be entitled to transfer the shares to the proposed transferee on terms no more favourable than those notified to the Company.

### 7.4 Shareholders' Agreements

Any shareholders' agreement entered into by some or all of the shareholders is binding only on its signatories. Such an agreement does not bind the Company or any third party, is not enforceable against the Company, and shall not be registered in, or otherwise noted on, the Company's shareholder register, unless and to the extent its terms are also set out in these Articles of Association.

### 7.5 Register

The board of directors shall ensure that the shareholder register is updated upon valid transfer.

### 9.2 Appointment

### 9.3 Rules of Procedure

### 9.4 Employee Board Representation

If the Company, together with any group companies, employs, on average over the period prescribed by the Danish Companies Act, a number of employees in Denmark that triggers a statutory right to board representation (currently at least 35 employees on average over the preceding three years), the employees of the Company and its group companies shall be entitled to elect members and alternates to the board of directors in accordance with the Danish Companies Act and the applicable rules on employee representation. If and when such right is triggered, these Articles of Association shall be amended to the extent necessary to reflect the employees' representation on the board of directors.

## 14. Amendments

### 14.1 Majority

Amendments to these Articles of Association require a resolution passed by at least two-thirds (2/3) of both the votes cast and the share capital represented at the general meeting, unless a higher majority or additional requirement applies under the Danish Companies Act (Selskabsloven) or these Articles of Association.

### 14.2 Increased Obligations

Notwithstanding Section 14.1, any resolution that increases the obligations of the shareholders towards the Company requires the unanimous approval of all shareholders, in accordance with the Danish Companies Act.

## 15. Dissolution

### 15.1 Resolution

The Company may be dissolved by resolution of the general meeting, adopted with the majority set out in Section 14.1, in accordance with the Danish Companies Act.

### 15.2 Distribution Waterfall

Upon dissolution and liquidation of the Company, the net assets remaining after settlement or provision for all liabilities shall be distributed among the shareholders in the following order of priority, consistent with and subject to any liquidation preferences or other class rights recorded under Section 5.2 and Schedule 1:

1. **first**, to the holders of any share class carrying a liquidation preference under Schedule 1, up to the liquidation preference amount specified for that class, applied in the order of priority set out in Schedule 1;
2. **second**, to the holders of any other share class carrying a specified preferential or fixed distribution right under Schedule 1, in accordance with the terms set out there; and
3. **thereafter**, the remaining balance, if any, shall be distributed pro rata among all shareholders in proportion to their shareholdings, unless Schedule 1 provides otherwise.
