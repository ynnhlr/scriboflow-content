## 2. Background

### 2.1 Creation

The Assignor has created or contributed to certain intellectual property.

### 2.2 Transfer Intent

The Parties wish to ensure that ownership of such intellectual property is transferred to the Company for the Company's intended use and exploitation of it in its business, including any modification, adaptation, and further development of it (the "Intended Exploitation").

## 3. Definitions

"Intellectual Property" means all rights in and to:
- inventions
- patents and patent applications
- trademarks and trade names
- copyrights and related rights
- software and source code
- databases
- designs
- know-how and trade secrets
- any other proprietary rights

whether registered or unregistered.

## 4. Assignment of Rights

### 4.1 Assignment

The Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to:
- all Intellectual Property created in connection with **[Engagement or project]**
- all derivative works and improvements thereof

### 4.2 Scope

The assignment includes worldwide rights for the full duration of protection.

### 4.3 Future IP

The assignment applies to existing and future Intellectual Property created within the scope of engagement.

## 5. Moral Rights

### 5.1 Nature of Moral Rights

The Assignor may hold moral rights (droit moral) in copyright works comprised in the Intellectual Property under section 3 of the Danish Copyright Act (Ophavsretsloven), including rights of attribution and integrity. Such rights are personal to the author and, under Danish law, cannot be assigned and cannot be waived in blanket terms.

### 5.2 Non-Assertion

To the extent the Assignor holds such moral rights, the Assignor undertakes not to invoke or assert them against the Company's Intended Exploitation of the Intellectual Property, or any use reasonably related to it. This undertaking is limited to the Intended Exploitation and does not constitute a waiver of moral rights for any other purpose.

### 5.3 Third-Party Authors

Where any part of the Intellectual Property was created, in whole or in part, by a natural person other than the Assignor, the Assignor shall procure from that person, before or promptly after the Effective Date, a written undertaking on terms equivalent to Section 5.2.

## 6. Further Assurances

### 6.1 Cooperation

The Assignor shall execute any documents and take any actions necessary to perfect or register the Company's ownership.

### 6.2 Recordal of Registered Rights

Where any Intellectual Property is registered, or is capable of registration, including patents, trademarks, and designs, the Assignor shall execute all documents and provide all assistance reasonably requested by the Company to record this assignment with the Danish Patent and Trademark Office (Patent- og Varemærkestyrelsen), the Danish Business Authority, or other relevant national, EU, or international register. The Company shall bear the reasonable filing and administrative costs of such recordal.

### 6.3 Survival

The obligations in this Section survive termination of employment or engagement.

## 7. Consideration

The assignment is made:
- as part of the Assignor's employment or engagement, or
- in consideration of **[Number] [Currency]**

Unless otherwise required by law, no additional compensation shall be payable. This Section does not limit any non-waivable statutory right the Assignor may have to reasonable additional remuneration for an employee invention under the Danish Act on Employee Inventions (lov om arbejdstageres opfindelser), where the value of the invention significantly exceeds what could reasonably have been anticipated at the time of this assignment.

## 8. Pre-Existing Intellectual Property

### 8.1 Excluded IP

Intellectual Property developed prior to the Effective Date remains the property of the Assignor unless listed in Schedule 1 and expressly assigned.

### 8.2 License

If pre-existing Intellectual Property is incorporated into assigned work, the Assignor grants the Company a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to use such Intellectual Property.

## 9. Representations and Warranties

The Assignor represents that:
- the Assignor is the sole creator and owner of the assigned Intellectual Property
- the assigned Intellectual Property does not infringe third-party rights
- the Assignor has full authority to enter into this Agreement

## 10. Indemnification

### 10.1 Indemnity

The Assignor shall indemnify the Company against direct losses and reasonable costs arising from a breach of the representations in Section 9.

### 10.2 Claims Procedure

The Company shall notify the Assignor promptly in writing of any claim under this Section, provide reasonable details and supporting documentation, and give the Assignor a reasonable opportunity to participate in, and where the Assignor is not itself a defendant, to control at its own cost the defence or settlement of, any related third-party claim.

### 10.3 Cap and Time Limit

Except in cases of fraud or wilful misconduct, the Assignor's aggregate liability under this Section shall not exceed **[Number] [Currency]**, and no claim may be brought unless notified to the Assignor in writing within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the Effective Date. This limitation is intended in particular to protect against disproportionate and open-ended exposure under section 36 of the Danish Contracts Act (Aftaleloven) where the Assignor is an individual.

## 11. Confidentiality

The Assignor shall keep confidential all proprietary information relating to the assigned Intellectual Property. This obligation survives for as long as the information constitutes a trade secret or know-how under the Danish Trade Secrets Act (lov om forretningshemmeligheder), and, for all other confidential information, for a period of **[Number] [Time unit]** from the Effective Date.

## 12. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 13. Severability

If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

## 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the assignment of Intellectual Property. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 15. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
