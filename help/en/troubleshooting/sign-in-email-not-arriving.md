# When a sign-in email does not arrive

Scriboflow can send verification emails during signup and magic-link emails when you choose password-free sign-in. Delivery can sometimes take a moment.

## Check the address and inbox

Confirm that the email address shown in Scriboflow is correct. Then check the inbox, spam, junk, and filtered folders for that account. If you requested more than one email, use the most recent code or link.

## Request a new email

Use **Resend** on the verification page, or request another magic link from the sign-in page. Avoid sending many requests in quick succession, because Scriboflow limits repeated attempts to protect the account and email service.

## Try another sign-in method

If your account has a password, you can sign in with the email address and password instead. You can also use the password-reset flow if you no longer remember the password.

If the email still does not arrive, contact Scriboflow and include the address you are trying to use. Do not include your password or a verification code.
