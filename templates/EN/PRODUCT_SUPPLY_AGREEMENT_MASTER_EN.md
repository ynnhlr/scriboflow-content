## 2. Products

### 2.1 Product List

The Supplier agrees to supply the products described in Schedule 1 (the "Products").

### 2.2 Specifications

The Products shall conform to agreed specifications and applicable laws.

### 2.3 Changes

Any modification to specifications must be agreed in writing.

## 3. Term

### 3.1 Commencement

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date.

### 3.2 Initial Term

The Agreement shall continue for an initial term of **[Number] [Time unit]**, unless terminated in accordance with Section 13.

### 3.3 Renewal

Thereafter, it shall automatically renew unless terminated with **[Number] [Time unit]** written notice.

## 4. Orders

### 4.1 Purchase Orders

The Buyer shall submit purchase orders specifying quantities and delivery dates.

### 4.2 Acceptance

Orders become binding upon written confirmation by the Supplier.

### 4.3 Rejection

The Supplier may reject orders if production capacity or commercial conditions do not allow acceptance.

## 5. Pricing

### 5.1 Prices

Prices are set out in Schedule 2.

### 5.2 Adjustments

Prices may be adjusted upon **[Number] [Time unit]** written notice, provided that any adjustment:
(a) is tied to a named, publicly verifiable price index, or to a documented increase in the Supplier's underlying costs (such as raw material costs, exchange-rate movements, or regulatory compliance costs); and
(b) is supported, on the Buyer's request, by evidence of the underlying cost change or index movement relied upon.

If the Buyer does not accept a price increase, the Buyer may terminate this Agreement, or the affected Products, by written notice given within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the price-increase notice, effective no earlier than the date the increase would otherwise take effect.

### 5.3 Taxes

All prices are exclusive of VAT unless otherwise stated.

## 6. Payment Terms

### 6.1 Payment

Invoices are payable within **[Number] [Time unit]** from invoice date.

### 6.2 Late Payment Interest

Late payments accrue interest on the overdue amount from the due date until payment in full at a rate of **[Interest rate]** per annum.

### 6.3 Recovery Costs

In addition to interest under Section 6.2, the Supplier is entitled to the fixed compensation for debt recovery costs prescribed by the Danish Interest Act (Renteloven), implementing the EU Late Payment Directive, for each overdue invoice. This entitlement cannot be waived by the Buyer in advance.

### 6.4 Suspension

The Supplier may suspend deliveries in case of overdue payments.

### 6.5 Retention of Title

Title to the Products shall not pass to the Buyer until the Supplier has received payment in full for the Products concerned. This retention of title is agreed between the Parties before or at the time of delivery of each shipment. Until title passes:
(a) the Buyer shall keep the Products reasonably identifiable as belonging to the Supplier and separate from goods belonging to the Buyer or third parties, and shall not process, combine, or incorporate the Products into other goods in a manner that would destroy their identifiability, except with the Supplier's prior written consent;
(b) the Buyer may resell the Products to its own customers in the ordinary course of business before payment, provided that, upon the Supplier's request, the Buyer shall assign to the Supplier its claim against the sub-buyer arising from such resale, to the extent of the unpaid price; and
(c) this retention of title is a security arrangement securing payment for the specific Products delivered and shall not be construed as a disguised financing or leasing arrangement.

## 7. Delivery

### 7.1 Delivery Terms

Delivery shall take place in accordance with **[INCOTERM] [Location]**.

### 7.2 Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates unless expressly agreed as fixed.

### 7.3 Risk

Risk passes in accordance with the agreed Incoterm.

### 7.4 Partial Deliveries

Partial deliveries are permitted unless otherwise agreed.

## 8. Quality and Inspection

### 8.1 Inspection

The Buyer shall inspect Products promptly upon delivery.

### 8.2 Complaint Period

Complaints must be submitted in writing within **[Number] [Time unit]** of delivery.

### 8.3 Remedies

In case of defects, the Supplier may, at its discretion:
- repair
- replace
- refund defective Products

## 9. Compliance

### 9.1 Product Compliance

The Supplier warrants that Products comply with applicable safety and regulatory standards at the time of delivery.

### 9.2 Resale Compliance

The Buyer is responsible for compliance related to resale, labeling, and marketing within its territory.

## 10. Intellectual Property

### 10.1 Supplier IP

All intellectual property related to the Products remains the property of the Supplier unless otherwise agreed.

### 10.2 Buyer Specifications

If Products are manufactured according to Buyer's specifications, the Buyer warrants that such specifications do not infringe third-party rights.

## 11. Indemnification

### 11.1 Supplier Indemnity

The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against third-party claims arising from product defects caused by the Supplier.

### 11.2 Buyer Indemnity

The Buyer shall indemnify the Supplier for claims arising from misuse, improper storage, or unauthorized modification of the Products.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

### 12.1 Indirect Damages

Neither Party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages, including loss of profit.

### 12.2 Liability Cap

The Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total value of Products delivered during the preceding **[Number] [Time unit]**, except in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 13. Termination

### 13.1 Termination for Convenience

Either Party may terminate this Agreement with **[Number] [Time unit]** written notice.

### 13.2 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate immediately in case of material breach not remedied within **[Number] [Time unit]**.

### 13.3 Effects

Upon termination, all outstanding payments become due.

## 14. Force Majeure

### 14.1 Force Majeure Event

Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations (other than payment obligations, which are never excused by force majeure) due to events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, fire, governmental action, and significant and unforeseeable disruption to the Supplier's own supply chain that could not have been avoided by reasonable contingency measures. General market shortages, routine price fluctuations, or a supplier's own avoidable sourcing difficulties do not, by themselves, constitute force majeure.

### 14.2 Maximum Duration and Termination

If a force majeure event prevents the Supplier from delivering all or a material part of the Products for a continuous period exceeding **[Number] [Time unit]**, the Buyer may terminate this Agreement, or the affected orders, by written notice with immediate effect, without liability to the Supplier other than payment for Products already delivered.

## 15. Confidentiality

Each Party shall keep confidential all commercial and technical information received from the other Party.

## 16. Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent, except in connection with a merger or business transfer.

## 17. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

### 17.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules.

### 17.2 Exclusion of the CISG

The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) shall not apply to this Agreement or to any purchase order placed under it. The Danish Sale of Goods Act (Købeloven) applies as background law between the Parties, to the extent not otherwise provided in this Agreement.

### 17.3 Jurisdiction

Any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 18. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the supply of Products. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 19. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
