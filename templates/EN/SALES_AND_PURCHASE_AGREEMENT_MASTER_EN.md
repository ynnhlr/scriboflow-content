## 2. Sale of Assets

### 2.1 Sale

### 2.2 Excluded Assets

The sale includes only the Assets expressly listed. No other assets, liabilities, or obligations are transferred unless explicitly stated.

## 3. Purchase Price

### 3.1 Price

The total purchase price for the Assets is **[Number] [Currency]** (the "Purchase Price").

### 3.2 Payment

Payment shall be made:
- in full on the Closing Date, or
- in accordance with the payment schedule set out in Schedule 2

### 3.3 VAT and Transfer Characterisation

#### 3.3.1 VAT Treatment

The Purchase Price is exclusive of VAT unless otherwise specified. Before Closing, the Parties shall agree and document in writing whether the sale is treated as a VAT-exempt transfer of a going concern (virksomhedsoverdragelse) under the Danish VAT Act (Momsloven), or as a taxable sale of assets on which VAT must be charged. The agreed characterisation shall be applied consistently by both Parties in their invoicing and VAT reporting.

#### 3.3.2 Authority Disagreement

If the Danish Tax Agency (Skattestyrelsen) or any other competent authority determines that a different VAT treatment applies than the one agreed under Section 3.3.1, the Party whose position gave rise to the incorrect characterisation shall bear any resulting VAT, interest, and penalties, and shall indemnify the other Party for losses caused by that incorrect characterisation. Where the sale is recharacterised as taxable, the Buyer shall pay any additional VAT due against a valid invoice, unless the recharacterisation resulted from the Seller's inaccurate information.

## 4. Closing

### 4.1 Closing Date

Closing shall take place on **[Date]** (the "Closing Date").

### 4.2 Closing Deliveries

On the Closing Date:
- the Seller shall transfer ownership of the Assets
- the Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price
- the Parties shall execute any additional transfer documents required

## 5. Transfer of Risk, Title and Retention of Title

### 5.1 Risk

Risk of loss or damage to the Assets passes to the Buyer upon delivery of the Assets, regardless of when title passes under Section 5.2.

### 5.2 Title

Legal title to the Assets remains with the Seller and does not transfer to the Buyer merely by delivery, execution of this Agreement, or Closing. Title transfers only upon full and unconditional payment of the Purchase Price (including all instalments under Schedule 2, if applicable), unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### 5.3 Conditions for Retention of Title

The retention of title in Section 5.2 is agreed as of the Effective Date, prior to delivery of the Assets, so that it is valid against the Buyer's other creditors and on the Buyer's insolvency under Danish law. For the retention of title to remain effective:
- the Assets must remain clearly identifiable as the Seller's property and must not be commingled with other assets in a way that destroys their identity;
- the Buyer shall not sell, pledge, encumber, lease, or otherwise dispose of the Assets, and shall not process or incorporate them into other property, before full payment, without the Seller's prior written consent, except with such consent in the ordinary course of the Buyer's business;
- if the Buyer resells or otherwise disposes of Assets subject to retention of title with the Seller's consent before full payment, the Buyer's claim against the relevant third party for the resulting proceeds is hereby assigned to the Seller to the extent of the unpaid Purchase Price, and the Buyer shall promptly notify the Seller of any such disposal and provide reasonable assistance to perfect that assignment.

### 5.4 Insurance

Until title passes under Section 5.2, the Buyer shall keep the Assets adequately insured against loss, theft, and damage, and shall, if requested, name the Seller as loss payee to the extent of the unpaid Purchase Price.

### 5.5 Insolvency

If insolvency, restructuring, or similar proceedings are initiated in respect of the Buyer before title has passed, the Buyer shall promptly notify the Seller and shall not obstruct the Seller's right to recover the Assets in accordance with Section 5.2.

## 6. Representations and Warranties

### 6.1 Seller Representations

The Seller represents and warrants that:
- it has full authority to sell the Assets
- the Assets are free from liens and encumbrances unless disclosed in Schedule 1 (the "Title Warranties")
- to its knowledge, the Assets do not infringe third-party rights

### 6.2 Buyer Representations

The Buyer represents and warrants that:
- it has authority to enter into this Agreement
- funds are available to pay the Purchase Price

## 7. No Assumption of Liabilities

The Buyer does not assume any liabilities of the Seller unless explicitly stated in this Agreement or in Section 8 (Employee Transfer).

## 8. Employee Transfer

### 8.1 Automatic Transfer

The Parties acknowledge that if the Assets constitute, in whole or in part, a business or undertaking (or part of one) to which employees are assigned, the employment relationships of those employees transfer automatically to the Buyer by operation of the Danish Act on the Legal Position of Employees on the Transfer of Undertakings (Virksomhedsoverdragelsesloven), regardless of any provision of this Agreement to the contrary.

### 8.2 Disclosure

If Section 8.1 applies, the Seller shall, no later than **[Number] [Time unit]** before the Closing Date, provide the Buyer with a list of the affected employees (the "Transferring Employees") together with their material terms of employment, seniority, accrued but unused holiday entitlement, pension commitments, and details of any pending or threatened employment disputes.

### 8.3 Allocation of Liabilities

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Seller remains liable for all obligations relating to the Transferring Employees that accrued before the Closing Date (including accrued but unpaid holiday pay, bonuses, and notice-related claims), and the Buyer assumes obligations relating to the Transferring Employees accruing on or after the Closing Date.

### 8.4 Notification and Consultation

The Parties shall comply with any applicable information and consultation obligations under the Virksomhedsoverdragelsesloven and shall cooperate in good faith on the timing and content of any required notices to the Transferring Employees or their representatives before Closing.

### 8.5 Indemnities

The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer for liabilities relating to the Transferring Employees accruing before the Closing Date that were not disclosed under Section 8.2. The Buyer shall indemnify the Seller for liabilities relating to the Transferring Employees accruing on or after the Closing Date.

### 8.6 No Employees Transferred

If the Assets do not constitute a business or undertaking with employees assigned to it, this Section 8 shall not apply and no employees transfer as a result of this Agreement.

## 9. Indemnification

### 9.1 Seller Indemnity

The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer against losses arising from breach of representations and warranties.

### 9.2 Buyer Indemnity

The Buyer shall indemnify the Seller for losses arising from misuse of the Assets after Closing.

### 9.3 Claims Period

Claims for breach of representations and warranties, other than the Title Warranties or claims based on fraud, must be made in writing within **[Number] [Time unit]** after Closing. Claims for breach of the Title Warranties and claims based on fraud are not subject to this claims period and may be brought within the general limitation periods under the Danish Limitation Act (Forældelsesloven).

## 10. Limitation of Liability

### 10.1 Indirect Damages

Neither Party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

### 10.2 Liability Cap

Except as set out in Section 10.3, the Seller's total liability shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in cases of fraud or wilful misconduct.

### 10.3 Carve-Outs from the Cap

The cap in Section 10.2 does not apply to:
- breach of the Title Warranties, for which the Seller's liability shall not be limited by Section 10.2, and the Buyer shall in addition be entitled to seek specific performance or equivalent relief necessary to secure clear title to the Assets;
- fraud or wilful misconduct;
- any liability of the Seller for taxes or duties allocated to the Seller under Section 3.3, or for employee-related liabilities allocated to the Seller under Section 8.

## 11. Confidentiality

The Parties shall keep the terms of this Agreement confidential unless disclosure is required by law.

## 12. Taxes and Costs

Unless otherwise agreed:
- each Party bears its own legal and advisory costs
- transfer taxes or registration fees shall be borne by **[Party reference]**, subject to Section 3.3 regarding VAT

## 13. Termination

### 13.1 Termination Before Closing

This Agreement may be terminated prior to Closing by mutual written agreement.

### 13.2 Effects

If terminated before Closing, neither Party shall have further obligations except for confidentiality.

## 14. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

### 14.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules. The Parties confirm that the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) shall not apply to this Agreement. To the extent the Assets include goods, the Danish Sale of Goods Act (Købeloven) shall apply as background law to any matter not expressly regulated by this Agreement.

### 14.2 Jurisdiction

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 15. Severability

If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

## 16. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the sale of the Assets and supersedes prior negotiations or agreements. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 17. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.
