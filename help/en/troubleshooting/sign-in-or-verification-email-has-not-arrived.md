# A sign-in or verification email has not arrived

Email delivery can take a few minutes. Request one message, then complete these checks before requesting several more links.

## Check the address and inbox

Confirm that the email address entered in Scriboflow is complete and belongs to the account you intend to use. Then:

1. Wait a few minutes and refresh the inbox.
2. Check spam, junk, promotions and quarantine folders.
3. Search the mailbox for “Scriboflow”.
4. Check whether a workplace mail filter has held the message.
5. Ask your mail administrator to allow expected Scriboflow messages if necessary.

Do not forward a sign-in or verification link to another person. It is intended for the account that requested it.

## Request a fresh email

Return to the Scriboflow sign-in or verification page and request a new message once. Use the newest email because older links may expire or stop being current after another request.

Open the link in the browser where you intend to use Scriboflow. If you have several accounts, confirm that the address matches the intended organisation invitation or workspace.

## Contact support

If no message arrives, contact support with the email address you entered, the approximate request time, your mail provider or business domain, and whether any Scriboflow email reached spam or quarantine. Include the visible error message and browser if the request itself failed.

Never send support your password, one-time code or the active sign-in link. For invitation-specific issues, see [An organisation invitation is missing or expired](/help/troubleshooting/organisation-invitation-is-missing-or-expired).
