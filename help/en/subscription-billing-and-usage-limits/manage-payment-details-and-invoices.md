# Manage payment details and invoices

Scriboflow uses Stripe to manage payment details and invoices for paid subscriptions.

Only the organisation owner can open its billing portal:

1. Open **Settings** and select **Billing**.
2. Select **Manage subscription**.
3. Select **Payment details**.

The Stripe billing portal opens in a new page. From there, you can update the payment method and review or download invoices for the organisation.

The payment option becomes available after the organisation has completed its first checkout.

If the Billing page shows **Payment issue**, Stripe could not collect the latest invoice. Update the payment details to help keep the organisation's access active.
