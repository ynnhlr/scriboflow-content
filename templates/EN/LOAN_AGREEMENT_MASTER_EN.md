## 6. Early Repayment

## 7. Covenants

### 7.1 Negative Pledge

The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Lender, create, incur, permit to exist or agree to create any mortgage, charge, pledge, lien, business charge (virksomhedspant) or other security interest or encumbrance over any of its present or future assets, other than:
- security granted to the Lender under this Agreement;
- any security interest existing and disclosed to the Lender as of the Effective Date and listed in Schedule 1; and
- security interests securing indebtedness not exceeding **[Number] [Currency]** in aggregate at any time.

### 7.2 Cross-Default

The Borrower shall be in default under this Agreement if the Borrower or any Guarantor:
- fails to pay when due any other indebtedness in excess of **[Number] [Currency]**; or
- is declared to be in default, or has any other indebtedness declared due and payable prior to its stated maturity, under any other agreement for borrowed money,

provided that this Section 7.2 shall not apply where the relevant failure or declaration is being contested in good faith by appropriate proceedings and adequate provision has been made for it.

### 7.3 Reporting

The Borrower shall deliver to the Lender, at intervals of **[Number] [Time unit]**, its financial statements and such other financial or operational information as the Lender may reasonably request in order to monitor the Borrower's financial position and its compliance with this Agreement.

## 8. Default

### 8.1 Events of Default

The Borrower shall be in default if:
- any payment is overdue by more than **[Number] [Time unit]**
- the Borrower becomes insolvent
- the Borrower breaches material obligations under this Agreement, including the covenants set out in Section 7 (Covenants)
- a cross-default occurs as described in Section 7.2
- the Borrower breaches the negative pledge undertaking in Section 7.1

### 8.2 Default Interest

Upon the occurrence of an event of default, the outstanding principal (and, to the extent permitted by law, unpaid accrued interest) shall bear default interest at the rate of **[Interest rate]** per annum from the due date until actual payment, in place of the statutory default interest rate under the Danish Interest Act (renteloven). Default interest **[shall compound / shall not compound]**; where it compounds, unpaid default interest shall be added to the outstanding principal at intervals of **[Time unit]** and shall thereafter itself bear default interest at the same rate. The agreed default interest rate remains subject to the fairness/moderation provisions of the Danish Contracts Act (aftaleloven).

### 8.3 Lender Remedies

Upon default, the Lender may:
- declare the entire outstanding principal and accrued interest immediately due and payable
- claim default interest in accordance with Section 8.2
- enforce any security in accordance with Section 9 (Security) and any guarantee in accordance with Section 10 (Guarantee)
- initiate legal recovery proceedings

## 9. Security (Optional)

### 9.1 Security Provided

As security for repayment, the Borrower provides:
- **[Personal guarantee]**
- **[Pledge over assets]**
- **[Security type]**

### 9.2 Perfection of Security

The Borrower shall, at its own cost and without undue delay, complete all steps required under Danish law to perfect, and to maintain the perfection of, the security granted under this Agreement so that it is valid and enforceable against third parties and on the Borrower's insolvency, including, as applicable:
- registration of any charge over real property with the Danish Land Registry (tinglysning);
- registration of any business charge (virksomhedspant) in accordance with the rules on virksomhedspant under the Danish Land Registration Act (tinglysningsloven);
- notice (denuntiation) to the relevant account-holding bank of any pledge over bank accounts or receivables, together with the bank's written acknowledgment of such notice; and
- any other registration, notice, filing or transfer of possession required to perfect the relevant security interest.

The Borrower shall provide the Lender with evidence of completion of the above formalities within **[Number] [Time unit]** of the Effective Date, or of the date the relevant security is granted, whichever is later.

### 9.3 Priority Ranking

The security granted to the Lender under this Agreement shall rank **[Priority ranking]** (e.g., first priority) as against the claims of other creditors of the Borrower, save for any prior-ranking security specifically identified in Schedule 1. The Borrower shall not grant any subsequent security ranking ahead of the Lender's security without the Lender's prior written consent.

### 9.4 Enforcement

The Lender may enforce security in accordance with applicable law upon default.

## 10. Guarantee (Optional)

### 10.1 Guarantee Scope and Cap

Where a Guarantor is named in Section 1, the Guarantor unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to the Lender the due and punctual payment and performance of the Borrower's obligations under this Agreement, up to a maximum aggregate amount of **[Number] [Currency]** (the "Guarantee Cap"), covering principal, interest (including default interest under Section 8.2) and recoverable costs under Section 12.

### 10.2 Duration

The guarantee shall remain in effect from the Effective Date until the earlier of (a) full and final discharge of the Borrower's obligations under this Agreement, and (b) **[Expiry date]**, unless extended or released earlier by written agreement of the Lender.

Where the Parties instead elect to document the guarantee in a separate instrument, the guarantee shall be set out in a separate guarantee deed between the Lender and the Guarantor (the "Guarantee Instrument"), which shall form part of this Agreement by reference and shall be subject to the same terms as to cap, duration and subrogation as set out in this Section 10.

### 10.3 Guarantor Representations

The Guarantor represents and warrants to the Lender that:
- the Guarantor has full legal capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement (or the Guarantee Instrument, as applicable);
- the guarantee constitutes a legally binding and enforceable obligation of the Guarantor;
- the Guarantor is not insolvent at the time of signing; and
- the Guarantor has received a copy of this Agreement and understands the extent of the guarantee given.

### 10.4 Subrogation

Upon making any payment to the Lender under this guarantee, the Guarantor shall be subrogated to the Lender's rights against the Borrower to the extent of such payment, provided that the Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, indemnity or contribution against the Borrower for as long as any amount remains outstanding or owing by the Borrower to the Lender under this Agreement.

## 11. Representations and Warranties

### 11.1 Borrower Representations

The Borrower represents that:
- it has authority to enter into this Agreement
- the Agreement constitutes a legally binding obligation
- it is not insolvent at the time of signing

## 12. Costs

The Borrower shall bear reasonable costs related to enforcement of this Agreement in case of default.

## 13. Assignment

The Borrower may not assign rights or obligations under this Agreement without written consent. The Lender may assign, transfer or otherwise dispose of its rights under this Agreement, including in connection with refinancing or a business transfer, provided that any assignment of the Lender's rights shall include a corresponding assignment or transfer of the security granted under Section 9 (Security) and any guarantee granted under Section 10 (Guarantee). The assignee shall, at its own cost, complete all registration, notice (denuntiation) and other formalities required under Section 9.2 (Perfection of Security) so that the transferred security remains valid, perfected and enforceable against third parties, and the Lender shall notify the Borrower and any account-holding bank of the assignment without undue delay.

## 14. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 15. Severability

If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

## 16. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the Loan. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 17. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original. Where a Guarantor is named in Section 1, the Guarantor shall countersign this Agreement (or, if the guarantee is instead documented in a separate Guarantee Instrument under Section 10.2, shall execute that Guarantee Instrument) as evidence of the Guarantor's acceptance of the guarantee obligations set out in Section 10.
