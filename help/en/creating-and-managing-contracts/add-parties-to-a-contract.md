# Add parties to a contract

Parties are the companies or individuals involved in an agreement. Each party can have one or more people assigned as signees.

## Add an existing party

<!-- help-image:parties -->
![Parties](../../assets/add-parties-to-a-contract/parties-v1.webp)

1. Open the contract's **Parties** step.
2. Select **Add another party**.
3. Search the available parties by company, name or email.
4. Select the party you want to add.

The saved party details are added to this contract. You can also set the wording used to refer to the party in the agreement.

## Create a new party

<!-- help-image:create-party -->
![Create party](../../assets/add-parties-to-a-contract/create-party-v1.webp)

If the party is not already available, choose **Create new party**. Add the relevant company or personal details, then save the party. It becomes available to the contract and can be reused in future contracts.

## Assign signees

<!-- help-image:assign-party -->
![Assign party](../../assets/add-parties-to-a-contract/assign-party-v1.webp)

Select a saved signee for each party or create a new one. Every party must have at least one signee before you can continue.

Check names and email addresses carefully. These details are used later when Scriboflow prepares and sends the signing requests. You can change the signing order in the **Signature** step.

To remove a party from the current draft, use **Remove party** on its card. This does not delete the saved party from your organisation.
