## 7. Holidays and Leave

### 7.1 Holiday Entitlement

The Employee is entitled to five weeks (25 days) of paid holiday per year in accordance with the Danish Holiday Act (ferieloven). Holiday accrues concurrently with its use (samtidighedsferie): holiday earned in a given month is available for use from the beginning of the following month, in accordance with the Act.

### 7.2 Statutory Leave

The Employee is entitled to statutory leave, including sickness, parental leave, and other protected leave, subject to applicable law.

### 7.3 Illness Reporting

Absence due to illness must be reported without undue delay in accordance with company procedures.

### 7.4 Settlement on Termination

Upon termination of employment, any holiday accrued but not taken shall be settled in accordance with the Danish Holiday Act, including by payment of accrued holiday allowance (feriepenge) to the Employee's holiday account (Feriekonto) or directly by the Employer, as applicable under the Act. Accrued but unused holiday shall not be forfeited by reason of this Agreement.

## 8. Confidentiality

### 8.1 Confidential Information

The Employee shall not disclose confidential information concerning the Employer's business, clients, suppliers, or employees.

### 8.2 Survival

This obligation continues after termination of employment without limitation in time.

## 9. Intellectual Property

### 9.1 Ownership

All work products, inventions, materials, and intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of employment shall belong exclusively to the Employer to the extent permitted by law.

### 9.2 Assignment of Economic Rights

The Employee assigns to the Employer all transferable economic rights in such intellectual property, without additional compensation unless otherwise required by law.

### 9.3 Moral Rights

Certain rights of authorship, including the right to be identified as author and the right to object to derogatory treatment of a work, are personal moral rights under section 3 of the Danish Copyright Act (ophavsretsloven) and cannot be assigned or waived in full. To the extent necessary for the Employer's ordinary use of the work within its business, the Employee waives the exercise of such moral rights against the Employer, without prejudice to the non-waivable core of those rights under Danish law.

## 10. Non-Competition and Non-Solicitation (If Applicable)

### 10.1 Eligibility

A non-compete clause and/or a customer non-solicitation clause may only be imposed on the Employee if the Employee occupies a position of special trust or has access to information of a genuinely sensitive nature to the Employer, in accordance with the Danish Act on Employment Clauses (ansættelsesklausulloven). If applicable, the specific clause(s), including the trust/sensitivity basis relied on, are set out in Schedule 2.

### 10.2 Statutory Maximum Duration

Any non-compete clause, and any combined non-compete and customer non-solicitation clause, applicable under Schedule 2 may not exceed 12 months from the date of termination of employment.

### 10.3 Customer Clause Look-Back

A customer non-solicitation clause may only apply to customers with whom the Employee, or persons the Employee is responsible for, has had a business connection within the 12 months preceding the Employer's or Employee's notice of termination.

### 10.4 Compensation

Where a restriction under this Section 10 applies after termination of employment, the Employer shall pay the Employee mandatory monthly compensation for the duration of the restriction, calculated and payable in accordance with the Act on Employment Clauses, as further specified in Schedule 2.

## 11. Secondary Employment

## 13. Data Protection

## 14. Termination

### 14.1 Statutory Notice Floor

Notice of termination is subject to the mandatory minimum notice periods under section 2 of the Danish Salaried Employees Act (funktionærloven), calculated from the Employee's start date to the date notice is given:

**Employer's notice (minimum):**
- Up to 6 months of employment: 1 month
- 6 months up to 2 years and 9 months: 3 months
- 2 years and 9 months up to 5 years and 8 months: 4 months
- 5 years and 8 months up to 8 years and 7 months: 5 months
- More than 8 years and 7 months: 6 months

**Employee's notice (fixed):** 1 month, irrespective of seniority.

- Employee notice: **[Number] [Time unit]**

### 14.3 Immediate Termination

The Employer may terminate the employment without notice in case of gross misconduct or material breach, subject to applicable law.

### 14.4 Garden Leave (If Applicable)

The Employer may require the Employee not to perform duties during the notice period while continuing salary payment.
