# Do drafts count toward the contract allowance?

No. A contract that remains a draft does not count towards your organisation's contract allowance.

## When a contract counts

The contract begins to count after its first successful external send. That is the point where Scriboflow sends the contract to an external recipient for review or signature. Creating, editing, previewing or signing your own draft without an external send does not use the allowance.

Each contract counts once. Resending its signing link, sending a revised version through the supported changes flow or changing its status does not create another allowance use for the same contract.

## Status changes do not restore usage

After a successful external send, later outcomes do not give the allowance use back. The contract still counts if it is signed, rejected, voided, archived or returned with requested changes. Deleting or archiving it is therefore not a way to restore usage.

This policy describes which contract event counts. Your available quantity and reset period depend on your organisation's subscription. Review the current usage and plan information in Scriboflow for those values.

For the complete policy and examples, see [Understand contract allowances](/help/subscription-billing-and-usage-limits/understand-contract-allowances).
