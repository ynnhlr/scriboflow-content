# Accept an organisation invitation

An organisation owner or administrator can invite you to their Scriboflow workspace. Accept the invitation with the email address it was sent to so Scriboflow can connect the correct account.

## Before you accept

Check that the organisation name and invited email address are the ones you expect. If you already use Scriboflow, sign in with that address. If you do not have an account, follow the invitation to create one with the same address.

Invitations expire after seven days. Use the most recent invitation email if you received more than one, and do not forward the link to another person.

## Join the organisation

1. Open the invitation email from Scriboflow.
2. Select the invitation link.
3. Sign in or create your account with the invited email address.
4. Select **Accept invitation**.
5. Confirm that the organisation appears in your organisation menu.

You join with the Member role. The organisation owner can change your role if your responsibilities require different access.

## If the invitation does not work

Make sure you are signed into the invited account, then try the latest link again. If it is expired, revoked or already used, ask an owner or administrator to resend the pending invitation or create a new one.

Tell them the email address you are using and the organisation name. Do not send the invitation link itself. For more checks, see [Troubleshoot team invitations and access](/help/team-members-and-permissions/troubleshoot-team-invitations-and-access).
