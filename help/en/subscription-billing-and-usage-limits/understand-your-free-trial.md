# Understand your free trial

A new Scriboflow organisation starts with a free 14-day Pro trial. You do not need to add a payment card to begin the trial.

During the trial, the organisation can use the features and limits included with Pro.

## Check when your trial ends

<!-- help-image:image -->
![Billing](../../assets/understand-your-free-trial/image-v1.webp)

Open **Settings** and select **Billing**. The **Current billing** section shows the trial status and end date.

## Continue after the trial

<!-- help-image:image-1 -->
![Plans](../../assets/understand-your-free-trial/image-1-v1.webp)

The organisation owner can select a paid plan before the trial ends:

1. Open **Settings** and select **Billing**.
2. Select **Manage subscription**.
3. Choose a billing interval and plan.
4. Complete the checkout.

If no plan is selected, the organisation becomes read-only when the trial ends. Existing contracts and files stay available, but you cannot create or send new contracts until the owner selects a plan.
