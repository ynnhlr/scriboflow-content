## 2. Appointment

### 2.1 Exclusivity

The Supplier appoints the Distributor as its exclusive distributor for the Products described in Schedule 1 within the Territory defined in Section 3.

### 2.2 Supplier Restrictions

During the term of this Agreement, the Supplier shall not appoint other distributors within the Territory nor actively sell directly into the Territory, subject to applicable competition law.

### 2.3 Distributor Undertaking

The Distributor accepts the appointment and undertakes to actively promote and sell the Products.

### 2.4 Independent Reseller Status

The Distributor purchases the Products from the Supplier and resells them in its own name and for its own account. The Distributor bears the full commercial risk of resale, including price risk and credit risk toward its customers, and has no authority to negotiate, conclude, or otherwise bind the Supplier to any contract with a third party. The Parties intend the relationship created by this Agreement to be that of an independent buyer and reseller, and not that of a commercial agent within the meaning of the Danish Commercial Agents Act (Handelsagentloven), notwithstanding the exclusivity, targets, and brand-control obligations set out in this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to entitle the Distributor to any goodwill compensation or indemnity payable to a commercial agent under that Act.

## 3. Territory

### 3.1 Territory

The Territory covered by this Agreement is **[Territory]** (the "Territory").

### 3.2 Active Sales Outside the Territory

The Distributor shall not actively sell the Products outside the Territory without the Supplier's prior written consent. "Active sales" means actively approaching individual customers by, among other means, direct mail, visits, or advertising and promotion specifically targeted at customers located outside the Territory.

### 3.3 Passive Sales

Passive sales into the Territory by third parties, and passive sales by the Distributor outside the Territory — that is, responding to unsolicited orders without specifically targeted marketing — can never be restricted, prevented, or penalised under this Agreement, regardless of the exclusivity granted to the Distributor. Restricting passive sales, or granting the Distributor absolute territorial protection against passive sales, is a hardcore restriction under applicable Danish and EU competition law and is not permitted.

### 3.4 Resale Prices

Any price list, recommended price, or suggested resale price communicated by the Supplier is non-binding on the Distributor. The Supplier shall not fix, require, or otherwise seek to impose minimum or fixed resale prices on the Distributor. Such conduct, together with any attempt to grant the Distributor absolute territorial protection against passive sales, constitutes a hardcore restriction capable of voiding any applicable competition-law exemption for the entire Agreement.

## 5. Performance Obligations

### 5.1 Distributor Obligations

The Distributor shall:
- maintain adequate inventory
- employ qualified sales personnel
- comply with brand and marketing guidelines
- meet minimum purchase targets set out in Schedule 2

### 5.2 Failure to Meet Targets

If the Distributor fails to meet the minimum purchase targets set out in Schedule 2, the Supplier may convert this Agreement to non-exclusive status or terminate this Agreement, but only where:
(a) the Supplier has given the Distributor prior written notice specifying the shortfall, measured against the objective criteria set out in Schedule 2;
(b) the Distributor has been given a cure period of **[Number] [Time unit]** to remedy the shortfall, or to demonstrate that the shortfall resulted from factors outside its reasonable control (including the Supplier's own conduct); and
(c) the shortfall persists at the end of that cure period.

### 6.2 Sales Contracts

### 7.3 Payment Terms

### 9.1 Compliance

### 9.2 Competition Law

The Parties shall comply with applicable competition and antitrust laws, including the rules on vertical agreements under Danish and EU competition law, and in particular the passive-sales and resale-price rules in Sections 3.3 and 3.4.

## 10. Warranties and Liability

### 10.1 Product Compliance

### 10.2 Liability Terms

### 10.3 Indirect Damages

## 11. Term

### 11.1 Initial Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of **[Number] [Time unit]**.

### 11.2 Renewal

Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of the same duration unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **[Number] [Time unit]** before the end of the then-current term. This notice period is a minimum floor and shall be applied having regard to the duration of the relationship and the volume of trade built up by the Distributor, so that the Distributor is afforded a reasonable opportunity to wind down or reorganise its business before the Agreement ends.

## 12. Termination

### 12.1 Immediate Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately in case of:
- material breach not remedied within **[Number] [Time unit]**
- insolvency
- repeated failure to meet performance obligations, subject to the notice and cure process in Section 5.2 where applicable

### 12.2 Effects

Upon termination:
- outstanding payments become immediately due
- the Distributor shall cease representing itself as exclusive distributor
- remaining inventory arrangements shall be agreed in writing

## 13. Goodwill Compensation

Unless required by mandatory law, the Distributor shall not be entitled to compensation for goodwill or loss of business upon termination.

## 14. Confidentiality

Each Party shall treat confidential information received from the other Party as confidential.

## 15. Force Majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform due to events beyond reasonable control.

## 16. Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent, except in connection with a merger or business transfer.

## 17. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

### 17.1 Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Denmark, excluding conflict-of-law rules.

### 17.2 Exclusion of the CISG

The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) shall not apply to this Agreement or to any sales contract concluded under it. The Danish Sale of Goods Act (Købeloven) applies as background law between the Parties, to the extent not otherwise provided in this Agreement, and the Parties acknowledge that, as merchants, most of its provisions may be and are hereby varied by this Agreement.

### 17.3 Jurisdiction

Any dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of **[Location]**, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.

## 18. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the exclusive distribution of the Products. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

## 19. Execution

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
