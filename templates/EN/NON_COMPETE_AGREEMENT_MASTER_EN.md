## 3. Non-Compete Obligation

### 3.1 Restricted Activities

During the Restricted Period, the Restricted Party shall not, directly or indirectly:
- engage in activities that compete with the Company's core business
- own, manage, control, or be employed by a competing business
- assist or advise any competing entity in a role similar to that performed for the Company

### 3.2 Passive Ownership

Passive ownership of publicly traded shares not exceeding **[Number]%** shall not constitute a breach.

## 4. Scope of Restriction

### 4.1 Activity Scope

The restriction applies only to activities substantially similar to those performed by the Restricted Party during the last **[Number] [Time unit]** of engagement.

### 4.2 Geographical Scope

The restriction applies within **[Territory]**.

### 4.3 Restricted Period

The non-compete obligation applies for **[Number] [Time unit]** following termination, which period shall in no event exceed 12 months, being the maximum period permitted under the Danish Act on Employment Clauses. Where the Restricted Party is simultaneously subject to a customer non-solicitation restriction under Section 8 or a separate agreement (a combined clause), the aggregate duration of the non-compete and customer non-solicitation restrictions together shall likewise not exceed 12 months.

## 5. Compensation

### 5.1 Mandatory Monthly Compensation

In consideration for the non-compete obligation under Section 3, the Company shall pay the Restricted Party compensation for every month of the Restricted Period, in accordance with the Danish Act on Employment Clauses, calculated as a percentage of the Restricted Party's monthly salary at the time of termination:
- **40%** per month, where this Agreement is a stand-alone non-compete clause; or
- **60%** per month, where this Agreement operates together with a customer non-solicitation clause covering the same or an overlapping period (a combined clause).

The applicable basis for this Agreement is: **[Specify: stand-alone non-compete / combined non-compete and non-solicitation]**. Compensation shall be paid monthly in arrears for the entire Restricted Period, regardless of whether the Restricted Party is placed on garden leave.

### 5.2 Mitigation

Where the Restricted Party obtains other suitable employment or income during the Restricted Period, the Company may deduct such other income from the compensation otherwise payable, but the Company shall in no event reduce the compensation below:
- **16%** of the monthly salary at the time of termination, for a stand-alone non-compete clause; or
- **24%** of the monthly salary at the time of termination, for a combined clause,
in accordance with the minimum floor prescribed by the Danish Act on Employment Clauses for the clause type specified in Section 5.1.

## 6. Garden Leave (Optional)

The Company may place the Restricted Party on garden leave during the notice period, during which salary shall continue and competitive activities remain prohibited.

## 7. Confidential Information

Nothing in this Agreement limits the Restricted Party's ongoing obligation to maintain confidentiality of the Company's proprietary information.

## 8. Customer Non-Solicitation (Optional Clause)

### 8.1 Restriction

For a period of **[Number] [Time unit]** following termination (subject to the 12-month maximum, and the combined 12-month maximum, described in Section 4.3), the Restricted Party shall not:
- solicit or attempt to solicit customers of the Company with whom the Company had a business connection at any time during the 12-month period immediately preceding the date on which notice of termination of the employment or engagement was given (the "Protected Customers")
- solicit or hire the Company's employees

### 8.2 Compensation for Customer Non-Solicitation

Where this clause is maintained as a combined clause together with the non-compete obligation in Section 3, it is compensated as part of the combined-clause percentage set out in Section 5.1. Where it applies as a stand-alone customer non-solicitation restriction (i.e., without an accompanying non-compete obligation), the Company shall instead pay compensation of **24%** of the Restricted Party's monthly salary at the time of termination, for each month of the restriction, subject to the same mitigation floor logic as Section 5.2.

### 9.1 Reasonableness

### 9.2 Severability

## 10. Remedies

### 10.1 Injunctive Relief

The Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in case of breach.

### 10.2 Damages

The Company may claim damages in accordance with applicable law.

## 11. Lapse and Termination of Restriction

### 11.1 Voluntary Waiver

The Company may waive the non-compete obligation in writing, in which case compensation obligations shall cease in accordance with applicable law.

### 11.2 Automatic Lapse on Employer-Initiated Termination

Where the Company terminates the Restricted Party's employment or engagement for reasons not attributable to the Restricted Party, or the Restricted Party terminates the relationship as a result of the Company's material breach, any non-compete and/or customer non-solicitation obligation under this Agreement shall lapse automatically as of the date the termination takes effect, unless the Company confirms to the Restricted Party in writing, no later than **[Number] [Time unit]** after notice of termination is given, that it wishes to maintain the restriction (in whole or in part). Such confirmation shall specify which restriction(s) are maintained and, once given, shall trigger the Company's obligation to pay the compensation set out in Section 5 (and, if applicable, Section 8.2) for the restriction so maintained.
