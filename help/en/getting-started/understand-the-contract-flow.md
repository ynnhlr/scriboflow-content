# Understand the contract flow

Scriboflow keeps the document, participants and activity together as a contract moves from preparation to completion. Its status tells you where it is in that flow.

## Prepare the draft

A new contract starts as a draft. You can write it in the editor, use a template or import an existing document. Add the parties and signees, complete required data fields, attach supporting files and review the terms.

Drafts remain editable and do not count towards the contract allowance. Use **Preview** to check the complete recipient experience before you send anything externally.

## Send and collect responses

When you send the contract, Scriboflow emails each recipient a secure signing link. If signing order is enabled, recipients receive access in sequence. Otherwise, they can act without waiting for another signee.

Recipients can review and sign the contract, request changes or reject it. The contract status and activity history show what has happened. A first successful external send counts towards the organisation's contract allowance; later status changes do not remove that use. See [Understand contract allowances](/help/subscription-billing-and-usage-limits/understand-contract-allowances) for the full policy.

## Complete and manage the contract

After every required signee has signed, the contract becomes signed. You can download the completed PDF and its signature certificate, and keep the agreement in the organisation's contract archive.

Continue to use Scriboflow to find the contract, review its activity and follow relevant dates. If a recipient requests changes, open the contract and review the request before deciding what to update and resend. For all available statuses, see [Understand contract statuses](/help/creating-and-managing-contracts/understand-contract-statuses).
